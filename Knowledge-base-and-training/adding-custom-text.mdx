---
title: "Adding Custom Text"
---

Add information directly when it doesn't exist in files or websites.

## **How to add custom text**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **Text**
2. Paste or type your content (at least 10 characters)
3. Choose your Doubles and folder
4. Click **Add to knowledge base**

## **What to know**

- Treated as a knowledge source, not instructions. It adds facts, not behavioral rules
- Clear, structured text improves answer quality
- Can be edited or replaced anytime
- Text can't be shared with visitors as a file. It stays inside the Double

## **Best for**

- FAQs
- Summaries
- Personal opinions
- Your approach or methodology
- Product details and pricing
