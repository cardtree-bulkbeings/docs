---
title: "Connecting Google Drive"
---

Sync files from your Google Drive. You choose exactly which files to sync, so you stay within your training words.

## **How to connect Google Drive**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **Google Drive**
2. Connect your Google account in the popup
3. Browse or search your files, and filter by file type
4. Select the files you want and sync them

## **What to know**

- Works with docs, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, and text files
- You can connect multiple accounts (for example, personal and work) and switch between them

## **Keeping files up to date**

- Use **Sync Now** for an instant sync
- Turn on automatic updates to pull in changes
- Use **Remove from KB** to take a synced file out of your Knowledge
