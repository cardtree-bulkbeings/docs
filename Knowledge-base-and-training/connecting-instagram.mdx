---
title: "Connecting Instagram"
---

Import posts from an Instagram account to train the AI Double on your content.

## **How to connect Instagram**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **Instagram**
2. Enter an @username or a post link
3. Choose an **Import size**, where available
4. Review the estimated cost, then start the import

## **What to know**

- Imports posts authored by the account, and can transcribe video
- Only public accounts and posts are accessible
- Paste a direct link to one post to import only that post
- Imports use credits. The cost is shown before you start, and the final cost can be lower. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- Instagram sources can update automatically with auto-refresh

## **Managing Instagram connection**

Open the source's menu in your library to refresh it, check for new posts, set up auto-refresh, or **Delete** it.
