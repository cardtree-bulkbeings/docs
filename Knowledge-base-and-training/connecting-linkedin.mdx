---
title: "Connecting Linkedin"
---

Import a LinkedIn profile, company page, or posts to train the AI Double on professional content and insights.

## **How to connect LinkedIn**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **LinkedIn**
2. Enter a profile or company URL, or a handle
3. Choose what to import:
   - **Posts** to import posts
   - **Profiles** to import the full profile and background data
4. Choose an **Import size**, where available
5. Review the estimated cost, then start the import

## **What to know**

- Only public profiles and posts are accessible
- Paste a direct link to one post to import only that post
- Imports use credits. The cost is shown before you start, and the final cost can be lower. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- LinkedIn sources can update automatically with auto-refresh

## **Best practices**

Connect LinkedIn to:

- Share professional insights or expertise
- Post industry commentary or analysis
- Reference thought leadership in AI responses
- Build personal brand on LinkedIn

## **Managing LinkedIn connection**

Open the source's menu in your library to **Refresh profile**, check for new posts, set up auto-refresh, or **Delete** it.
