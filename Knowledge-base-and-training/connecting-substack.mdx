---
title: "Connecting Substack"
---

Import posts and articles from a Substack publication to train the AI Double on your writing.

## **How to connect Substack**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **Substack**
2. Enter the publication username or URL, for example `yourname`, `yourname.substack.com`, or `substack.com/@yourname`
3. Choose your Doubles and folder
4. Start the import

## **What to know**

- The latest public posts are imported, up to 25
- Paid posts aren't supported
- Substack sources don't auto-refresh. To pick up new posts, delete the source and add it again
- Your Double can share the publication link with visitors if sharing is on

## **Managing Substack connection**

To remove it, open its menu in your library and click **Delete**.
