---
title: "Connecting TikTok"
---

Import TikTok videos to train the AI Double on your videos, captions, and what you say in them.

## **How to connect TikTok**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **TikTok**
2. Enter an @username or a video link
3. Choose an **Import size**, where available
4. Review the estimated cost, then start the import

## **What to know**

- Imports videos, captions, and spoken content
- Only public accounts and videos are accessible
- Paste a direct link to one video to import only that video
- Imports use credits. The cost is shown before you start, and the final cost can be lower. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- TikTok sources can update automatically with auto-refresh

## **Managing TikTok connection**

Open the source's menu in your library to refresh it, check for new posts, set up auto-refresh, or **Delete** it.
