---
title: "Connecting YouTube"
---

Import YouTube videos. They are transcribed so the AI Double can learn from what you say in them.

## **How to connect YouTube**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **YouTube**
2. Paste your video links
3. Review the estimated cost, then start the import

## **What to know**

- Only public videos can be imported
- Videos are transcribed and the transcript is added to your Knowledge
- You are charged only for videos processed successfully. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- YouTube sources don't auto-refresh. Import new videos as you publish them

## **Best practices**

Connect YouTube to:

- Share talks, tutorials, and explainers
- Reference interviews and podcasts
- Capture expertise you've explained on video but not written down

## **Managing YouTube connection**

To remove a video, open its menu in your library and click **Delete**.
