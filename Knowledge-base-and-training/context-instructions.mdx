---
title: "LLM Knowledge (Context Instructions)"
---

LLM Knowledge, previously called Context Instructions, gives your Double the essential background it should draw on when answering: your work, expertise, and focus areas.

## **How to add LLM Knowledge**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **LLM Knowledge**
2. Write your professional baseline and key context (up to 4,000 characters)
3. Choose your Doubles
4. Click **Save instructions**

## **What to know**

- **4,000 character limit** - Keep it focused and clear
- **Facts and context only** - It doesn't set tone, style, or personality. Use Persona for those
- **Stays inside the Double** - It can't be shared with visitors as a file
- **Can be updated anytime** - Edit or replace it whenever your context changes

## **Best practices**

Use LLM Knowledge to add:

- Specialization or niche focus
- Industry or market
- Key background not in other sources
- Important context about approach
- Details that help qualify conversations

**Example:** "I'm a corporate lawyer specializing in intellectual property. Focus on legal precedents and case law when providing context."

## **Managing LLM Knowledge**

Find it in your library, open its menu, and click **Edit** to update the text.
