---
title: "Does Social Media Auto-Update?"
---

Yes, if you turn it on. LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok sources can now refresh automatically on a schedule you choose.

## **How to turn on auto-refresh**

1. Go to **Knowledge**
2. Find the social source in your library and open its menu
3. Click **Enable auto-refresh**
4. Pick a frequency: every 7, 15, 30, 60, or 90 days
5. Click **Enable auto-refresh** to confirm

New or changed posts are checked and indexed on that schedule.

To stop it, open the menu and click **Pause auto-refresh**.

## **How to refresh by hand**

Open the source's menu and use:

- **Refresh now** to update it straight away
- **Import more** to import older posts that weren't included yet
- **Check for new posts** once all earlier posts are imported
- **Refresh profile** for LinkedIn profiles and company pages

## **What to know**

- Each refresh uses your credits at the current rate. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- YouTube and Substack don't auto-refresh. Import new videos or posts as you publish them

## **When to refresh**

Turn on auto-refresh, or refresh by hand, when:

- You post often
- Your expertise or focus has evolved
- Your AI needs to reference recent insights
