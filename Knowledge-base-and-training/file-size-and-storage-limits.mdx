---
title: "What are the file size and storage limits?"
---

Knowledge capacity is measured in training words, and depends on your Double AI plan.

## **Check your limits**

1. Go to **Knowledge**
2. Your current usage appears under **Training words** at the top
3. [View pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing) for plan details

## **File limits**

- **Documents:** 30 MB per file
- **Images:** 10 MB per file
- **LLM Knowledge:** 4,000 characters

## **Training words by plan**

| **Plan** | **Stored knowledge** |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 100K words |
| Lite | 250K words |
| Pro | 1M words |
| Power | 5M words |
| Business | 20M words |

If you reach your limit, delete old sources or upgrade your plan.

**Tip:** With Google Drive, you choose exactly which files to sync, so you only use training words on what you need.
