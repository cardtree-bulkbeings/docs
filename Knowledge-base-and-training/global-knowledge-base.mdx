title: "Global Knowledge Base"
---

The global Knowledge Base is your central data library. Upload once, assign to any Double, so you're not re-uploading the same files for every profile.

Go to **Knowledge** from the main sidebar to get started.

**Training words** at the top of the page shows how much of your plan's capacity you've used.

### **Data Sources**

Click **All sources** to browse every source by category, or search for one. The sources you used last appear first as quick tiles.

| **Category** | **Source** | **What it does** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Files | **Upload Documents** | PDF, Word, CSV, and text files |
| Files | **Text** | Paste custom knowledge in text |
| Files | **Image** | Charts, diagrams, and visual content |
| Web | **Website URL** | Websites, docs, and public pages |
| Web | **Substack** | Posts and articles from a publication |
| Social | **LinkedIn** | Profile, company page, or posts |
| Social | **X / Twitter** | User timeline posts and replies |
| Social | **Instagram** | Posts authored by the account |
| Social | **TikTok** | Videos, captions, and spoken content |
| Social | **YouTube** | Videos transcribed and ingested |
| Apps | **Google Drive** | Docs, Sheets, and files from your Drive |
| Apps | **Notion** (Soon) | Pages and databases from a workspace |
| AI | **LLM Knowledge** | Add background context for your AI |

Every source form lets you choose which Doubles get it (**Assign to**) and which folder it is saved in (**Save to**).

### **Knowledge Library**

All your uploaded data sources live here. Use it to manage, organize, and assign content across your Doubles.

#### **What you can do**

- **Search** - find any source using the search bar
- **New folder** - organize sources into folders, then **Move** them between folders
- **Filter by type** - filter by source type
- **Filter by Double** - see which sources are assigned to which Double
- **Select** several rows to assign, move, or delete them together

#### **What to know**

- A source doesn't reach a Double until it is assigned. Assign it from the library, or use the **Assign to** option when you add it
- To see what's currently training a specific Double, go to **Your Double → Knowledge**. Use **Add from Library** there to pick folders or files
- While a file is processing, the library shows how far along it is
