---
title: "Managing Knowledge Sources"
---

Edit, update, or remove knowledge sources to keep the AI Double's information accurate and current.

## **Viewing sources**

All your sources live in one table in **Knowledge**. Search for any source, or filter by source type or by Double.

## **What you can do with a source**

Open a source's menu to:

- **Assign** it to one or more Doubles
- **Move** it to another folder
- **Rename**, **Edit**, or **Delete** it
- **Retry** it, if it failed
- Turn on **Sharing**, so your Double can send the file to visitors
- **Present** a PDF, to start a new Presentation skill from it

## **How to update sources**

- **Text and LLM Knowledge:** Edit them directly
- **Files and images:** Delete the old version and upload the new one
- **Websites:** Delete the source and add the updated URL
- **Social sources:** Use auto-refresh, or refresh by hand from the source's menu
- **Google Drive:** Use **Sync Now** or automatic updates

## **How to delete sources**

Open the source's menu and click **Delete**. To delete several at once, select their rows first. Your Double stops using that information.

## **Best practices**

- Review sources regularly to keep information current
- Remove outdated files and websites
- Set auto-refresh on social accounts you post to often
- Use folders to assign related sources together
