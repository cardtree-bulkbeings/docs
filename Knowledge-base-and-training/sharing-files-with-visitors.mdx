---
title: "Sharing Files with Visitors"
---

Knowledge your Double answers from isn't automatically something it can hand over. You choose what can be shared, and your Double sends the file itself, not just answers from it.

## **How to turn on sharing**

1. In your **Knowledge** library, open a source's menu and click **Sharing**
2. Turn on **Share with visitors**
3. In **When should Double share it?**, describe the situation in plain language. No exact keywords are needed
4. Click **Save**

**Note:** When you upload files, **Double can share these files** is on by default. Untick it to keep them private.

## **What can be shared**

| **Source** | **How it's shared** |
| --- | --- |
| **PDFs and images** | Sent as a file link |
| **Websites and Substack** | Sent as a link to the page |
| **Word, CSV, and text files** | Used for answers, never sent |
| **Text and LLM Knowledge** | Stay inside the Double |
| **Published presentations** | Marked shareable automatically |

## **What to know**

- **A shared file link works for an hour, and anyone who has it can open it**
- The **Used by** panel shows which Doubles and skills use the file
- The **Share assets** skill in **Skills** uses the same sharing settings, so you can manage shared files from either place
- In Google Meet, Google Chat, and WhatsApp, your Double shares files as links in the conversation
