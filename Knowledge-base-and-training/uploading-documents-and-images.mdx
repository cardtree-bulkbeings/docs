---
title: "Uploading Documents and Images"
---

Upload documents and images to train the AI Double on your files.

## **How to upload**

1. Open **Knowledge** and click **Upload Documents** or **Image**
2. Drop your files, or click to choose them
3. Choose your Doubles and folder
4. Click **Upload**

## **Supported files**

| **Type** | **Formats** | **Max size** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Documents** | PDF, Word (.doc, .docx), CSV, and plain text (.txt) | 30 MB per file |
| **Images** | JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and BMP | 10 MB per file |

## **What to know**

- You can upload multiple files at once
- Only searchable text inside a document is used
- Large documents are split into sections for better answers
- Images are read by AI, which writes a text description of them. This works best for charts, diagrams, infographics, and screenshots with text
- An image's thumbnail appears in chat citations
- **Double can share these files** is on by default, so your Double can send PDFs and images to visitors. Untick it to keep the files private. Word, CSV, and text files are used for answers but never sent
