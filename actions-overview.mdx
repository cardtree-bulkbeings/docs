title: "Actions Overview"
---

Actions let your Double do things — not just answer questions. You build a capability once, then decide which Doubles can use it.

There are five kinds of action:

| Action | What it is | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Skills** | A single capability, such as a role play, an interview, or sharing files | **Library → Skills** |
| **Tools** | Connected apps and what your Double may do with them | **Library → Tools** |
| **Routines** | Scheduled or triggered jobs Assist runs for you | **Assist → Routines** |
| **Presentations** | Slide decks your Double presents in your voice | **Library → Skills → Presentation** |
| **Workflows** | Advanced multi-step automations that chain apps together | `/app/actions/workflow` |

## Skills

Skills are the main way to teach your Double a specific job:

- **Role play** — practice conversations with feedback
- **Interview** — structured questions and responses
- **Share assets** — hand files and links to visitors
- **Presentation** — present a deck and answer questions
- **Custom** — your instructions plus tool access

## Tools

Tools are the apps your Double can act in — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, Shopify, and MCP servers. Connect an app once, choose which Doubles may use it, and set what each action can do.

## Routines

Routines run on a schedule or when something happens — for example, *"every Friday, email me the top questions."* Describe one in Assist and it sets it up.

## Presentations

Upload a deck and your Double presents it slide by slide, in your voice. Presentations are created from **Skills → Presentation**.

## Workflows

Workflows chain multiple actions, apps, and conditions together. They are the advanced option for multi-step logic; most day-to-day automation belongs in Skills and Routines.
