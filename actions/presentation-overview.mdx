---
title: "Presentation Overview"
---

Presentation is a skill that lets your Double present slide decks and answer questions on them. Add your deck, your Double drafts the rest, you review it, and you choose which Doubles use it.

**What has changed:** Presentations now live under **Skills**, in the same editor as other skills. One deck can be used by several Doubles at once. If you open the old Actions → Presentations address, you are redirected.

**What Presentations do**

- Read your deck and draft trigger phrases and a delivery instruction
- Present slide by slide, in your Double's voice, in chat and on voice
- Answer questions about the slides
- Work on your Double page, your website, and in Google Meet
- Share as a standalone link, or embed on your own site

**Best for**

- Product demos and pitches
- Sales presentations and case studies
- Training or educational walkthroughs
- Webinars and live presentations
