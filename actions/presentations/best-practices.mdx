---
title: "Best Practices"
---

**Structure your deck**

- 5-10 slides for quick demos
- 15-20 slides for in-depth walkthroughs
- One concept per slide
- Avoid text-heavy slides, because your Double speaks the content
- Use visuals to support spoken content

**Write effective instructions**

- Be specific: "When they ask to see the pricing tiers" (not just "demo")
- Keep it brief (1-2 sentences)
- Use a slide AI instruction when one slide needs special handling
- Update instructions based on conversation patterns

**Engage viewers**

- Use the first slide's script to set expectations: "This 5-minute walkthrough covers..."
- Bridge transitions: "Now, let me show you the dashboard"
- End with a next step: "Ready to get started? I can walk you through setup"
- Offer Q&A: "Any questions? I can dive deeper into any feature"

**Test before sharing**

- Run through the full presentation on the **Test** tab
- Test on mobile and desktop
- Share the link with your team for feedback
