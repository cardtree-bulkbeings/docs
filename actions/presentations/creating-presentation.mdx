---
title: "Creating a Presentation"
---

**Step 1: Add your deck**

1. Go to **Skills**
2. Click **Presentation**
3. Choose the Doubles that will use it
4. Drop in a PDF or PowerPoint, or click **Or choose a PDF already in Knowledge**
5. Optionally add a **Name** and **What is this deck for?**
6. Click **Add deck**

Your Double reads the deck, which takes a minute or two. You can carry on elsewhere, and you'll be told when it is ready.

**What to know**

- Supports .pdf, .pptx, and .ppt, up to 50 MB
- Google Slides → Download as PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF
- Under 20 slides works best
- If the name is blank, it defaults to the file name
- You can also start from a PDF in your Knowledge library: open its menu and click **Present**

**Don't have the deck yet?** Click **Set up without a deck**. You can set everything else up now, and it's kept when the deck arrives. You can't publish until the deck is added.

**Step 2: Review the Overview**

On the **Setup** tab, check what your Double drafted:

- **When should Double use this?** Describe it in plain language. No exact wording is needed. For example: "When someone asks about the villa project or wants to see the brochure"
- **What it does** A short description of the deck
- **Also start when someone says** Exact phrases for a guaranteed start. You can add up to 8, of up to 100 characters each. At least one is needed to publish

**What to know**

- Your Double uses these to decide when to offer the presentation
- You can edit them anytime

**Step 3: Review slides and behaviour**

Check the script for each slide, and how your Double should deliver the deck. See Slide Scripts and AI Instructions.

**Step 4: Test it**

Open the **Test** tab, or click **Test this presentation**. Choose a Double to test with, and try it the way a visitor would. Testing opens once the deck has been read.

**Step 5: Publish**

Click **Publish**. To publish, a presentation needs:

- A deck that has been read
- At least one trigger phrase
- At least one Double
