---
title: "Embedding Presentations on Your Website"
---

Go to **Setup → Share and embed**, and click **Create link and embed code**.

**Option 1: Standalone link**

1. Click **Copy** next to **Link**
2. Share it with visitors, prospects, or your team

The link format is `https://thedouble.ai/presentation/[token]`.

**Option 2: Embed on your website**

1. Under **Embed code**, choose **HTML** or **React**
2. Click **Copy code**
3. Paste it where you want the presentation on your page
4. Adjust the width and height as needed
5. Save, publish, and test on desktop and mobile

**What to know**

- Visitors can open the link and embed once the presentation is live. You can create them before that
- Website builders that accept custom HTML can use the **HTML** code
- Keep `allow="microphone; camera"` in the code so voice works
- Set the height explicitly, for example `height="600px"`
