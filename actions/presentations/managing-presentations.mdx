---
title: "Managing Presentations"
---

### **View all presentations**

Go to **Skills** to see all your decks alongside your other skills, with their status and the Doubles using them.

| **Status** | **What it means** |
| --- | --- |
| **Live** | Published and in use |
| **Draft** | Not published yet |
| **Paused** | Not being offered by your Double |
| **Processing** | The deck is being read |
| **Needs deck** | Set up without a file |
| **Failed** | The deck couldn't be read. Upload it again |

**Edit a presentation**

1. Open the presentation from **Skills**
2. Edit the **Setup** tab
3. Edits save automatically as a draft
4. Click **Republish** to update the live presentation

**Change which Doubles use it**

Change the Doubles at the top of the page. This takes effect straight away, with no need to republish. Edits to a deck apply to every Double using it.

**Replace a deck**

1. In the **Slides** section, click **Replace deck**
2. Choose **Upload a file** or **Choose from Knowledge**
3. Confirm the replacement

Scripts and slide instructions stay with their slide numbers. If the new file has fewer slides, any for slides that no longer exist are removed, and you'll see which ones. A live presentation can't present for a minute or two while the new file is read.

**Pause a presentation**

1. Click the **⋮** menu
2. Click **Pause presentation**
3. Your Double stops offering it
4. Click **Publish** to make it live again

**Delete a presentation**

1. Click the **⋮** menu
2. Click **Delete presentation**
3. It stops working on all its Doubles

**Results**

The **Results** tab for presentations is coming. For now, conversations that use a deck show up in your **Inbox**.

**In Knowledge**

Published presentations also appear in your Knowledge library, marked shareable.
