---
title: "Presentation Flow"
---

**Visitor initiates:**

1. Visitor asks about something the deck covers, or says one of your trigger phrases
2. Your Double opens the deck or suggests it
3. If a Double has several decks, it can offer a choice

**Presentation starts:**

1. Slide 1 appears beside the chat
2. Your Double explains it using your script and instructions
3. Visitor can read, listen, or both

**During presentation:**

- Visitor clicks **Next** and **Previous**, or asks your Double to move on, go back, or jump to a slide
- Your Double explains each slide using your script and instructions
- Visitor can ask questions at any time, and can type or talk
- Visitor can ask your Double to pause the deck and carry on chatting

**Presentation ends:**

1. Visitor reaches the final slide, or asks your Double to close the deck
2. Visitor returns to normal chat
