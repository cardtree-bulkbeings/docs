---
title: "Troubleshooting"
---

**Publish button is disabled**

- The message under the header says what is missing: a deck, a read deck, a trigger phrase, or a Double

**Presentation not starting**

- Check that the status is **Live**
- Check that your Double is selected under Doubles
- Make **When should Double use this?** clearer
- Test one of your exact trigger phrases

**Deck won't upload or read**

- Use a PDF, .pptx, or .ppt file under 50 MB
- If it shows **Failed**, try uploading it again or use a different file

**Voice not playing**

- Check your browser speakers and volume
- Check that the Double has a voice
- Check microphone permission in your browser
- Try a different browser

**Slides not displaying**

- Check that the deck finished processing
- Check the channel supports slides. Google Chat, Slack, and Phone can't show them
- Clear your browser cache and refresh
- Try another browser

**Embedded presentation not loading**

- Check that the presentation is **Live**
- Check that the iframe code is complete and includes `allow="microphone; camera"`
- Test in an incognito/private window, in case an ad blocker is interfering
