---
title: "Using Presentations in Chat"
---

**How visitors trigger presentations**

- **Your Double offers it:** a visitor asks about something the deck covers, and your Double opens or suggests it
- **Trigger phrase:** a visitor says one of your exact phrases
- **Direct link:** you share the presentation link directly with visitors

**During presentation**

- Visitors control slide navigation, or ask your Double to move between slides
- Visitors can ask questions at any time
- Voice conversations work (visitors can speak with your Double)

**Where presentations work**

| **Channel** | **How the deck appears** |
| --- | --- |
| Double page and website agent | Slides open beside the chat |
| Google Meet | Your Double shares the slides in the meeting |
| WhatsApp | Sent as a link to the presentation page |
| Google Chat, Slack, and Phone | Slides can't be shown |
