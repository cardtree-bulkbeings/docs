title: "Interview Skills"

An interview skill — also called a **Questionnaire** — asks a set of questions one at a time, records the answers, and saves each completed run so you can review or export it. Use it for lead intake, applications, screening, feedback, quizzes, or surveys.

**Where to find it:** **Library → Skills → Interview**, or open a Double → **Configure → Skills**.

## Write your questions

Open the question builder under **What Double collects**.

> Double asks these one at a time, in its own voice.

Click **Add Question** to pick a template or write your own. Each question can be:

| Type | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Text / Input** | A free-text answer |
| **Choice (Single)** | One option |
| **Choice (Multi)** | One or more options |
| **Date / Time** | A date or time |

Templates cover common fields such as name, email, phone, company, job title, website, availability, budget, and more. You can also search templates or add a custom question.

### Validation

Double detects what a question expects from its wording and validates the answer:

- Email
- Phone
- Name
- Address
- Website / URL
- Date / time

A question with validation shows a badge. If an answer doesn't fit, the visitor is asked again with a helpful example.

### Simple or scored

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Simple** | Records the answer; no points |
| **Scored** | Grades the answer and adds points |

Use **Set all type** to switch every question to **All Simple** or **All Scored** at once.

For scored questions:

- **Choice** — tick the correct option or options.
- **Free text** — write a **Correct Answer / Guideline** describing what counts as correct, and set **Points** (default 1).

Scoring is covered below.

### Required and Say Exactly

- **Required** — the visitor must answer before moving on.
- **Say Exactly** — Double uses your text verbatim rather than treating it as guidance.

### Closing reply

Add an optional **Closing Reply** shown after the last question:

> e.g. Perfect, that gives me what I need. What would you like to explore next?

Leave it empty for a natural default. The global **Say Exactly** switch controls whether the closing is delivered word-for-word.

## How visitors experience it

1. The skill is triggered by a start phrase, by Double deciding it fits, or by a workflow.
2. For a standalone skill, Double asks first — for example, *"I can help with 'Mock interview'. Would you like to start the questionnaire now?"*

3. The visitor answers one question at a time.
4. Choice questions show selectable options with a **Confirm** button.
5. Invalid answers are re-asked with a correction hint.
6. Optional questions can be skipped.
7. At the end, the visitor sees the closing reply and a completion message.

If the visitor says **cancel**, **stop**, **quit**, **never mind**, or similar, the questionnaire stops.

Interviews work in chat and voice. On voice, questions are spoken and answers are transcribed.

## How scoring works

- Only **scored** questions count towards the total.
- **Choice questions** are correct when the answer matches or includes a correct option. For multi-choice, any correct option earns full points.
- **Free-text questions** are correct when the answer matches the guideline exactly, or when Double's grader judges it correct against your guideline.
- Well-formed names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses are accepted automatically.
- Optional questions left blank are recorded as skipped and excluded from the total.
- The result shows **Score: X / Y** and a percentage.

## Results and export

Open the skill's **Results** tab:

> Every completed questionnaire, newest first.

Each response shows the date, status, score, and every question with the visitor's answer. Scored answers are marked **✓ Correct** or **✗ Incorrect**.

Click **Download Responses** to export a CSV with:

- Name, email, phone, date, score, and status
- A row per question with the answer, correct answer, points earned, and maximum points

## Triggers

- **Also start when someone says** — exact phrases for a guaranteed start.
- **When should Double use this?** — plain-language guidance for when the skill fits.
- **Workflow** — a questionnaire can be a step in a workflow, including a chat-start workflow that asks its first question at the start of a conversation.

## Tips

- Keep questions short and concrete.
- Use choice questions when you need consistent data.
- Score only the questions that have a clear right answer.
- Use **Required** for anything you can't process without.
- Add a closing reply that tells the visitor what happens next.
- Check the Results tab after a few real conversations and tighten unclear questions.

## What to know

- A questionnaire must have at least one question.
- Answers are saved to the skill's **Results** tab.
- A skill needs to be **Active** and assigned to a Double before visitors can use it.
- Question text determines validation, so rewording a question can change how answers are checked.
- Scored free-text answers use an AI grader when the answer isn't an exact match; a missing grader configuration means only exact matches count.
- CSV export includes the responses currently loaded in the view.

## FAQ

**Why didn't the questionnaire start?** Check that the skill is active, assigned, and matched by a start phrase or a clear request. Double may also ask the visitor for consent first.

**Can the visitor skip a question?** Only optional questions. Required questions are asked again.

**Why did the visitor get asked the same question twice?** An answer failed validation — for example, an invalid email address.

**Can I use the same questions in a workflow?** Yes. Questionnaire is available as a workflow step.

**Where do the answers go?** To the skill's Results tab, and to the CSV export.

**Can I change the questions later?** Yes. Changes apply to new responses; existing responses keep the answers they recorded.
