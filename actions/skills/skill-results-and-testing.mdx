---
title: "Results and Testing"
---

Once a skill is saved, two more tabs appear: **Results**, where you see what happened when people used it, and **Test**, where you try it yourself exactly as a visitor would.

**Where to find it:** open **Library → Skills** (or a Double's Skills), then open a saved skill.

**Note:** Both tabs stay disabled until the skill has been saved. The tooltip says **"Create the skill first"**. Custom skills show **"Available when custom skills launch"**.

## Testing a skill

1. Open a saved skill.
2. Click **Test**.
3. Click **Start test session**.
4. Reply as a visitor would.
5. Review the outcome in **Results**.

### Role play test

A role play test starts a **real session**:

> A real session. It shows up in Results like any other.

- The Double opens in character and responds turn by turn.
- **Restart** starts over.
- **Session ended** appears when the configured end condition is reached.
- The session, transcript, and score are recorded like any visitor session.

### Interview test

An interview test is an interactive walk-through:

> An interactive walk-through. Completed responses are saved to Results.

- Questions are asked one at a time.
- Required questions must be answered.
- Choice questions show selectable options.
- Answers are validated where the question expects an email, phone number, name, address, URL, or date.
- You can type `skip`, `pass`, or `next` for an optional question.
- Completing the walk-through saves a test response in **Results**.

## Results

What you see depends on the kind of skill.

### Role play results

Three summary cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sessions** | Total sessions |
| **Completed** | Sessions that reached an end condition |
| **Average score** | Mean overall score, or **—** when no session has feedback |

The **Sessions** table lists:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Session** | The session title |
| **Date** | When it ran |
| **Questions** | How many questions were asked |
| **Length** | Duration in minutes or seconds |
| **Score** | Overall score out of 100 |
| **Status** | Session status |

Empty state: *"No sessions yet. Try it yourself from the Test tab, or share your Double page."*

Sessions are created by visitors and by your own tests. Feedback is owner-only — visitors never see their score during the conversation.

### Interview results

The **Responses** tab lists every completed questionnaire, newest first.

Each response shows:

- When it was completed and its status
- A **Score: X / Y** pill, or **Unscored (Data Collection)**
- Every question with the visitor's answer
- **✓ Correct** or **✗ Incorrect** for scored questions
- **No answer provided** when a question was skipped
- **Score / Eval** when the answer was graded

Click **Download Responses** to export a CSV. The file includes:

- Name, email, phone, date, score, and status for each response
- A question-by-question breakdown with the answer, correct answer, points earned, and maximum points

Empty state: *"No responses recorded yet."*

### Custom skill results

Results for custom skills become available when the new agent runtime launches.

## What to know

- Test sessions are real sessions and appear in Results.
- Interview test responses are saved the same way as visitor responses.
- Role play feedback is generated at the end of a session and is visible only to you.
- The **Average score** uses sessions that have feedback; unscored sessions are not counted.
- CSV export covers the responses loaded in the view.
- Results belong to the skill, so unassigning a Double does not delete its history.

## FAQ

**Why can't I open Results or Test?** Save the skill first. Custom skills wait for the new runtime.

**Does a Test session affect my metrics?** Role play tests count as sessions, which is useful for checking the experience. Interview tests add a response.

**Can visitors see their scores?** No. Role play feedback is for the owner. Interview scores appear in your responses view.

**Where is the transcript?** Role play sessions store their transcript and feedback; open the session from its results. Interview answers are listed per response.

**How many responses can I export?** The CSV includes the responses currently loaded in the Results view.
