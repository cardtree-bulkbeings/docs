title: "Action Permissions"
---

Every tool action has its own rule: **who can use it** and **whether Double asks you first**. This is where you decide what your Double may do on its own, what needs your approval, and what it may do for visitors.

**Where to find it:** **Library → Tools → Manage** on a connected tool, then open **What your Double can do**.

## Two audiences

At the top of the permission panel, switch between:

| View | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **When you ask** | What your Double may do when you ask it directly |
| **When a visitor asks** | What your Double may do for people using your public Double |

You can allow an action for yourself but not for visitors — useful for anything that touches internal data.

## Set everything at once

Use **Set all** for a quick starting point:

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Read only** | Read actions allowed; writes and deletes off |
| **Read and write** | Reads and writes allowed; destructive actions stay locked |
| **All available actions** | Every available action allowed |
| **Custom** | Keep the individual choices you have made |

Then fine-tune individual actions.

## Per-action settings

For each action you can choose:

### Who can use it

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Nobody** | Blocks the action for everyone |
| **Only me** | Only you can trigger it |
| **Me and visitors** | You and public visitors can trigger it |

### Confirmation

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask me first** | Double prepares the action and waits for your approval |
| **Run without asking** | Double can run it directly |

**Destructive actions** — such as deleting email or cancelling an event — are **Locked** and cannot be enabled from the panel.

## Reading the table

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | The specific operation, such as *Send email* |
| **Type** | **Read**, **Write**, or **Delete** |
| **Who can use it** | The audience setting, in the owner view |
| **Visitor outcome** | What a visitor experiences, in the visitor view |
| **Confirmation** | Whether the action asks first, in the owner view |

In the visitor view, each action resolves to one of these outcomes:

| Visitor outcome | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Runs** | Allowed without confirmation |
| **Asks you first** | The visitor's request waits for your approval |
| **Not permitted** | Limited to you, so visitors can't use it |
| **Blocked** | You turned the action off |
| **Not available** | Destructive or locked until reviewed |

## Bulk editing

For tools with many actions:

- Search for an action by name.
- Filter by **All**, **Read**, **Write**, or **Delete**.
- Use **Select all N**, **Select N results**, or **Deselect visible** to apply a change to a group.

Bulk changes still respect locked destructive actions.

## Save

Click **Save permissions**. The panel shows **Saving…** and then **Permissions saved**.

If you switch accounts with unsaved changes, Double asks:

> Switch accounts and discard the unsaved permission changes?

## How the rules combine

- **Tool access** — the tool must be connected and available to that Double.
- **Action permission** — the action must be allowed for the audience.
- **Confirmation** — if set to ask first, the run waits for you even when the action is allowed.
- **Provider permissions** — the connected account's own access still applies.

All four must allow the action for it to run.

## What to know

- Permissions are shared across all accounts connected to the same tool.
- **Nobody** blocks the action for you and for visitors.
- **Only me** keeps the action internal to you.
- **Me and visitors** exposes it to people using your public Double.
- Destructive actions are locked for safety and cannot be turned on from the permissions panel.
- Some actions are read-only by design, such as Shopify's product and inventory reads.

## Examples

**Let your Double draft emails but never send them:**
Set *Send email* to **Only me** with **Ask me first**, and leave visitors at **Not permitted**.

**Let visitors book meetings, but only within your calendar rules:**
Set *Check availability* to **Me and visitors** with **Run without asking**, and keep *Cancel event* locked.

**Keep CRM writes internal:**
Set *Create lead* to **Only me** with **Ask me first**; set *Read CRM records* to **Me and visitors**.

## FAQ

**Why is an action greyed out?** It is destructive or locked until reviewed.

**A visitor was asked for approval — what do I do?** Open **Assist → Needs you**, review the request, and approve or cancel it.

**Can I allow an action for visitors but not for myself?** Yes. The two views are independent.

**Do permissions apply to routines too?** Yes. Routines also show their own action permissions, with per-run and daily limits.

**I changed permissions but the Double still can't use the tool.** Check that the Double has access to the tool and that the connected account is healthy.
