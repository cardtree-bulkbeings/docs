Your **Card page** is the visual, profile-first version of your Double — a smart digital profile visitors can browse at their own pace. It lives alongside your Agent page and shows the same identity, but as a layout of cards instead of a conversation.

- **Individual:** `thedouble.ai/{handle}/card`
- **Organization:** `thedouble.ai/{org}/{handle}/card`

Visitors reach it from your Agent page with the **Card** button, or directly from the link you share. Everything on the Card page is yours to arrange — widgets, layout, colours, fonts, and the link preview people see before they open it.

## Opening the Card builder

There are three ways in:

1. **Cards page** — open **Cards** from the sidebar, then click **Edit** on a card tile.
2. **Channels** — open your Double → **Channels** → **Card** → **Card builder** tab.
3. **Public Card page** — if you are signed in as the owner, click the floating **Edit** button.

The builder opens with three tabs across the top:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Card builder** | Arrange widgets and the page's appearance |
| **Double widget** | Configure the chat widget shown on the Card |
| **Settings** | Sharing, link preview and SEO, and visibility |

Use **Preview** (top right) to open the live Card in a new tab, and **Share** to share it. The preview toggle switches between **web** and **mobile** layouts.

## Arranging your Card

Your Card is a 4-column grid. You can change it as often as you like — the layout updates as you go.

- **Drag** a widget by its handle to move it around the grid.
- **Resize** a widget with the size buttons that appear on hover: **Wide**, **Small**, **Large**, **Extra Large**, **Tall**, or **Medium**.
- **Edit** a widget with the pencil icon to open its settings.
- **Style** a widget with the palette icon to set its **Widget Background** and **Text Color**. Backgrounds can be **Solid**, an **Image**, or **Custom**; **Text** sets the text colour.
- **Delete** a widget with the red trash icon. The Hero widget cannot be deleted.

Some widgets show a **Configure** overlay until they have content — click it to fill in the widget.

Unsaved widget changes prompt you before closing: for example, *"You have unsaved changes in the hero card. Are you sure you want to close?"*

## Changing the page appearance

Click **Customize** on the left rail to open **Page style**. It has five sections:

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Themes** | **Flat (Default)**, **Memphis**, **Glass**, **Neumorphism** |
| **Skins** | 15 preset background and widget colour pairs, **Tinted widgets**, and **Custom color** with **Widget (HEX)** and **Background (HEX)** |
| **Corners & Shadows** | **Corner Radius** presets (0, S, M, L, XL) and a **Shadow Depth** slider from 0–100% |
| **Background** | **Colour** (solid or gradient), **Pattern**, or **Image** (from the gallery or your own upload) |
| **Fonts** | **Font pairs (Heading + Description)** or separate **Heading** and **Description** fonts |

Click **Save changes** to apply appearance edits. If you close the panel with unsaved changes, you'll be asked to confirm.

## Card settings

Open **Settings** in the builder (or **Cards → ⋯ → Settings**). It covers:

### Link preview and SEO

This is what people see when your Card link is shared.

- **Fav Icon** — upload an icon for the browser tab.
- **Link Preview** — the image shown in link previews. Hover it and click **Refresh** to regenerate it from your Card.
- **Double link** — your handle, which sets the public URL.
- **Title** — up to 70 characters.
- **Description** — up to 400 characters.
- **Display in search engines. Eg: Google, Bing.** — allows search engines to index the Card.

Click **Save changes**. A preview image is generated automatically if you don't upload one.

### Visibility

Choose who can open your Card:

| Option | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone, including search engines |
| **Unlisted** | Only people with the link; not indexed by search engines |
| **Private** | Only you |

**Private** Cards require you to be signed in as the owner. Turning **Display in search engines** on sets visibility to **Public**; turning it off sets it to **Unlisted**.

### Double link

Your handle is shared by your Agent page and Card page. Click **Update Handle** to change it.

- 3–30 characters
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only
- Must be unique

You'll see **Handle is available** when the new handle can be used. Changing it changes your public URL.

## Sharing your Card

From **Settings → Share settings**, or the **Share** button, you can create:

- **Setup QR code** — pick a colour, add a **QR icon**, and download the QR as a PNG.
- **Setup email signature** — a signature with your profile picture, name, job title, company, website, country, phone, Double link, QR code, banner, and disclaimer. Download the signature image, copy its HTML, or add it to your email.
- **Meeting background** — a video-call background built from your profile details. Choose an image from the library or upload your own, then download it.

The **Share** dialog also gives you quick actions: share on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, or email; copy your **Double Link** or **Card Link**; save your QR code; and open **Visibility Settings**.

## Saving and publishing

There is no publish button — your Card is always live.

- Adding, editing, moving, resizing, and deleting widgets saves automatically.
- Page appearance and Card settings use **Save changes**.
- Changes are visible on your public Card as soon as they save.

## What to know

- One **Hero** widget per Card.
- A Card is available at `/{handle}/card`; your Agent page stays at `/{handle}`.
- The same handle powers both pages, so renaming it affects both links.
- On the Free plan, **Location** cards and image uploads have limits — you'll see an upgrade prompt when you reach them.
- Free Cards show a **Powered by Double** badge. Upgraded plans can remove it.

## FAQ

**Why can't I find my Card?** Check **Settings → Visibility**. A **Private** Card only opens for you, and an **Unlisted** Card needs the exact link.

**How do I change my Card link?** Open **Settings → Link preview and SEO → Double link**, enter a new handle, and click **Update Handle**.

**Can I have different links for my Agent page and Card page?** No — both use the same handle. The Card page adds `/card` to the end.

**My link preview is blank or outdated.** Open **Link preview and SEO**, hover the preview image, and click **Refresh**.

**Can I remove the "Powered by Double" badge?** It is removed on paid plans.

**Do I need to publish after editing?** No. Widget changes save automatically, and appearance or settings changes apply when you click **Save changes**.
