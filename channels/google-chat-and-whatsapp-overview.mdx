---
title: "Google Chat and WhatsApp Overview"
---

Connect Google Chat or WhatsApp Business so your Double can draft and send replies where your conversations already happen. Both have an inbox inside The Double, and you stay in control of what gets sent.

Go to **Your Double → Channels** to get started. To see every channel across all your Doubles, click **Channels** in the main sidebar.

### **How replies work**

- Your Double prepares a draft when a new message arrives
- You review it, edit it, and send it
- **Automatic replies** are off until you turn them on, one conversation at a time

### **Channels in this section**

| **Channel** | **Status** | **What it connects** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Chat** | Beta | Direct messages and spaces in your Google account |
| **WhatsApp Business** | In review | A WhatsApp Business Account through Meta |

Gmail, Google Meet, and your other channels have their own pages.


### **What to know**

- Your Double uses the same knowledge and Persona as everywhere else
- Your Double can share knowledge files as links in these conversations
- Presentations can be sent as a link on WhatsApp, but can't be shown on Google Chat
- Lead capture doesn't run on Google Chat or WhatsApp
- Both channels are available on every plan. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing) for how messages use credits
