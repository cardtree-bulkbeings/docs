---
title: "Automatic Replies in Google Chat"
---

Let your Double reply in a conversation without waiting for your approval.

## **How to turn on automatic replies**

1. Open the conversation's **Conversation settings**, or find it in **Google Chat settings**
2. Turn on **Automatic replies**
3. Confirm **Allow automatic replies?**

## **Safeguards for each conversation**

| **Setting** | **Options** |
| --- | --- |
| **Pause automatic replies** | Stops automatic replies while paused |
| **Wait between replies** | No minimum wait, 5, 15, or 30 minutes |
| **Quiet hours** | 10 PM–8 AM, 11 PM–7 AM, or 8 PM–8 AM |

## **What to know**

- New messages are checked about once a minute
- Messages during a pause, quiet hours, or a reply wait are skipped. Your Double doesn't send those replies later
- Messages sent before the setting saves don't get an automatic reply
- Automatic replies won't attach files
