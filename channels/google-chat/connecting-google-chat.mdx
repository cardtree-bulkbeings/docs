---
title: "Connecting Google Chat (Beta)"
---

Your Double works from your connected Google account, across direct messages and spaces.

## **How to connect Google Chat**

1. Go to **Your Double → Channels**
2. Click **Google Chat**
3. Click **Connect Google Chat** and sign in with your Google account
4. If the channel is off, click **Turn on** on the Channels page
5. Open your conversations from **Direct messages**, **Spaces**, or **Apps**

## **Managing accounts**

- To add a second Google account, click **Connect another account** in the settings
- To disconnect an account, use the same settings
