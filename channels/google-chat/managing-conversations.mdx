---
title: "Managing Google Chat Conversations"
---

Open **Manage Google Chat** in a conversation's menu to:

- Change space details
- Turn history on or off
- Change the target audience
- Change space permissions
- Manage members

**Note:** These settings change the real Google Chat conversation, and affect everyone in it. Google enforces your role and your organization's policy. Target audiences and space permissions apply to named spaces, not direct messages.
