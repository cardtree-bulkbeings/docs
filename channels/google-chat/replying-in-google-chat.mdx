---
title: "Replying in Google Chat"
---

Your Double drafts a reply when a new message arrives. You review it before it goes out.

## **How to reply**

1. Open a conversation
2. Wait for the draft to appear, or write your own
3. Edit it if needed. Click **Regenerate** for a new draft, or **Discard** to remove it
4. Click **Review and send**, then **Confirm and send**

You can also add an emoji reaction to a message, and open any conversation in Google Chat itself.

## **Start a new conversation**

Start a conversation from the chat list, and enter a Google account email and a first message. This sends immediately from your connected account.

## **If a send seems to fail**

If a send didn't return a confirmed result, click **Refresh before retry** and check the chat first. Retrying may create a duplicate.
