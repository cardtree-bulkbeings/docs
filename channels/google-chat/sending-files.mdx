---
title: "Sending Files in Google Chat"
---

Attach files to a reply, or let your Double find the right ones for you.

## **How to send files**

1. Click **Attach file**, or **Find files with Double**
2. Confirm the files with your message

## **What to know**

- Up to 3 files, 3 MB in total
- Supports PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, CSV, and image files
- Confirming shares the original files with everyone in the conversation
- Files are sent only after you confirm. Automatic replies won't attach files
