---
title: "Connecting WhatsApp Business"
---

**Note:** WhatsApp Business is rolling out and is in review. It may show as **Soon** and **In review** on your Channels page.

This connects a WhatsApp Business Account through Meta. It doesn't connect a personal WhatsApp account.

## **How to connect WhatsApp Business**

1. Go to **Your Double → Channels** and click **WhatsApp Business**
2. Choose how to connect:
   - **Connect WhatsApp Business** to use the number through Double only
   - **Keep using WhatsApp Business on my phone** to keep using the phone app too
3. In the Meta window, choose your business and authorize Double
4. Choose your **Business phone number**, which is used for inbox messages and replies
5. Turn on WhatsApp Business for this Double on the Channels page

## **If you are asked to finish Cloud API registration**

1. Verify the number by SMS or phone call in Meta first
2. Click **Register for Cloud API**
3. Enter a six-digit two-step verification PIN. Use your existing PIN, or create one if none is set

**Note:** This PIN isn't the SMS code, so save it somewhere safe. After Cloud API registration, the number is no longer active in the WhatsApp Business phone app. To keep using the phone app, use the **Keep using WhatsApp Business on my phone** option instead.

## **Managing your connection**

Go to WhatsApp **Settings → WhatsApp Connection** to:

- Change your business phone number
- **Reconnect with Meta**
- **Disconnect account**

## **What to know**

If your inbox shows that **Incoming webhook is not ready**, new conversations and drafts won't arrive. Contact support to get this resolved.
