**Note:** WhatsApp Business is rolling out and is in review. It may show as **Soon** and **In review** on your Channels page.

Create reusable messages, check Meta's approval, and send approved templates to opted-in contacts.

## **How to create a template**

1. Go to WhatsApp **Settings → Templates**
2. Click **Create template**
3. Fill in the template:
   - **Template name:** lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only
   - **Language code:** such as `en_US`, `en`, `hi`, or `ar`
   - **Category:** **Utility** for updates about an existing request or order, or **Marketing** for offers, promotions, and engagement
   - **Header (optional):** text only, up to 60 characters, no variables
   - **Message body:** use variables such as `Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} is ready for pickup.`
   - **Footer (optional)**
4. Add example values, using sample data and not private customer information
5. Click **Submit to Meta**

## **Sending a template**

A template can't be sent until Meta approves it. Refresh to check its latest status, then click **Use template** to send it.

## **What to know**

- Sending supports text headers, body variables, and footers
- Media and buttons aren't supported yet
- Meta messaging charges may apply
