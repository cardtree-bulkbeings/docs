---
title: "Replying on WhatsApp"
---

**Note:** WhatsApp Business is rolling out and is in review. It may show as **Soon** and **In review** on your Channels page.

Your Double drafts a reply when a new message arrives. You review it before it goes out.

## **How to reply**

1. Open a conversation
2. Wait for **Draft ready**, or write your own reply
3. Edit it if needed. Click **Regenerate** for a new draft, or **Discard** to remove it
4. Click **Review and send**, then **Confirm and send**

## **Automatic replies**

1. Open **Conversation settings** in a conversation
2. Turn on **Automatic replies**
3. Confirm **Allow automatic replies?**

Automatic replies stay off until this setting saves. You can turn them off for a conversation at any time.

## **Start a new conversation**

1. Open the new message dialog from the inbox
2. Enter the **WhatsApp number**, including the country code
3. Choose **Free text** or **Template**
4. For a template, fill in its values and confirm that **This contact has agreed to receive these WhatsApp messages**
5. Click **Send**

**Note:** Free text only sends to someone who messaged you in the last 24 hours. See The 24-Hour Window.
