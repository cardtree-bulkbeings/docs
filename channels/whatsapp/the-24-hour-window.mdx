title: "The 24-Hour Window"
---

**Note:** WhatsApp Business is rolling out and is in review. It may show as **Soon** and **In review** on your Channels page.

WhatsApp limits how businesses can message people.

| **Message type** | **When you can send it** |
| --- | --- |
| **Free text** | Only if this person messaged you in the last 24 hours |
| **Approved templates** | Anytime, to start a chat with anyone or message someone outside the 24-hour window |

## **What to know**

- Templates need Meta's approval before you can send them
- Meta messaging charges may apply to templates
- Only send templates to contacts who have agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from you
