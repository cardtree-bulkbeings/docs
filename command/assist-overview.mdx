**Assist** is your workspace-wide control surface. It is a conversation with your Double (or Doubles) that can look things up, take action, and tell you what needs your attention — across every Double you own.

**Where to find it:** **Assist** at the top of the sidebar (`/app/assist`). Older links use `/app/command` and still work.

## What Assist can do

- Answer questions using your Doubles, knowledge, tools, channels, data, and people.
- Do work across Doubles — for example, reply to a lead, update a setting, or set up a routine.
- Show you what is waiting on you in **Needs you**.
- Show what your Doubles and routines did in **Activity**.
- Set up and manage **Routines** — scheduled or triggered jobs.

Assist is not a per-Double settings page. Use it when the work spans Doubles or when you want to describe an outcome instead of finding the right page.

## The home screen

When no chat is open, Assist shows a briefing:

- A greeting — **Good morning**, **Good afternoon**, or **Good evening**.
- **What can I help you with today?**
- Three shortcuts:

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reach someone** | Send a message through a channel — website, chat, voice, WhatsApp, email, phone, or Google Meet |
| **Teach my Double** | Create a skill, add knowledge, or change how your Double behaves |
| **See ideas** | Browse things Assist can do for you |

Below the shortcuts, up to two current prompts appear — an open question from a visitor, or a recent activity item. Click one to open it.

## Chatting with Assist

Type in **What do you want done?** and press Enter. If no chat is open, Assist starts a new one.

- **Reply to Assist** is the placeholder once a conversation is open.
- **Nothing risky runs without your OK** — actions that change something ask for approval first.

### Add context with @

Type **@** to add a Double, tool, knowledge source, data set, person, saved work, or channel. Each item becomes a chip above the composer, labelled with its role:

| Role | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **target** | The Double the work is for |
| **source** | A tool or channel to use |
| **reference** | A knowledge source |
| **output** | Data or a deliverable |
| **recipient** | A person or team |
| **saved work** | A previous artifact |

If a tool is not connected yet, choosing it starts the connection flow instead of adding a chip. Adding the same item twice shows **already added** for that item.

### Attach files and dictate

- Click **+ → Attach a file**, paste, or drag a file into the composer.
- Audio files become a **Voice sample** chip; other files are added to knowledge.
- Click the mic to speak. Assist transcribes it with **Speak your message → Turning that into text… → Done speaking**.
- Attaching a file requires at least one Double.

## Approvals and cards

Assist can reply with more than text:

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Approval** | **Waiting for your approval** — review and choose **Approve** or **Cancel** |
| **Choose a Double** | Assist needs to know which Double to use |
| **Connect a channel / integration** | Finish sign-in in the pop-up window |
| **Billing** | Opens billing without changing anything |
| **Voice clone / voice sample** | Set up or use a cloned voice |
| **Public handle** | Choose the Double's public link |
| **Create a Double** | Start a new Double without leaving Assist |
| **Artifact** | A document, web page, graphic, or diagram with a version number |

Approvals are one-time. Once you approve or cancel, the card shows **Approved** or **Cancelled** and cannot be reused.

## Chats

Click **Chats** in the header to open your conversation history.

- **New chat** starts fresh.
- **Search chats** finds an older conversation.
- Conversations are grouped by **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last week**, and **Older**.
- Use the row menu to **Rename** or **Delete** a conversation.
- **Load more chats** pages through older threads.

A chat is titled automatically from your first request. Pending approvals are restored when you reopen the chat, and Assist can post a **Check-in** message while a conversation is open.

## The right-hand panel

On wide screens, Assist shows a panel with four tabs. On smaller screens, the same tabs open in a drawer.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | What needs you, plus recent activity |
| **Needs you** | Approvals, answers, fixes, and decisions |
| **Routines** | Scheduled and triggered jobs |
| **Activity** | Everything your Doubles and routines did |

Each tab has a **Filter and search** link to the full page.

## Finish in Double

If you use an AI app connected over MCP, it can send you to **Finish in Double** (`/app/command/handoff`) to complete a step only a browser can do — signing in to a channel or authorizing an integration.

1. Open the link from your AI app.
2. Click the provider button and finish sign-in in the pop-up.
3. Return to Assist; the page confirms the channel or integration is connected.
4. Go back to your AI app and continue.

If the link belongs to another workspace, you'll be offered **Switch workspace**.

## What to know

- Assist works across Doubles; use a Double's own sidebar for settings that belong to one Double.
- Assist can only use channels that are connected and allowed to send.
- Actions that change something ask first; read-only work runs without a prompt.
- Assist shows in-app updates only — there are no email or push notifications.
- Work that Assist sets up appears in **Routines**, and what it does appears in **Activity**.

## FAQ

**Is Assist the same as Command?** Yes. The feature was called Command; the interface now says Assist. Old `/app/command` links still open it.

**Why can't Assist use my Gmail?** Connect it in **Tools** first. Assist only uses tools that are connected and permitted.

**Why did Assist ask which Double to use?** Your request could apply to more than one. Pick one in the **Choose a Double** card.

**Can Assist delete or send things without asking?** Not by default. Risky actions require approval, and routine permissions control what can run without asking.

**Where did my chat go?** Open **Chats** and search for it. Chats are grouped by date.
