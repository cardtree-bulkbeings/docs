title: "Needs You and Activity"
---

Two Assist pages keep you in control of what your Doubles are doing:

- **Needs you** — anything a Double can't finish without your approval, answer, fix, or decision.
- **Activity** — a plain-language log of everything your Doubles and routines did.

**Where to find them:** **Assist → Needs you** and **Assist → Activity**, or the matching tabs in the Assist panel.

## Needs you

> Anything your Doubles can't finish without you: approvals, answers, fixes and decisions. Resolve one anywhere and it clears everywhere.

Open asks are sorted by urgency — **High**, **Medium**, then **Low** — and newest first.

### Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | **All**, **Approve**, **Answer**, **Fix**, **Decide** |
| **Doubles** | One Double, or all |
| **Source** | **Skills**, **Tools**, **Knowledge**, **Channels**, **Routines**, **Billing**, **Doubles**, **System** |
| **Priority** | **High**, **Medium**, **Low** |
| **Channel** | Website, Chat, Voice, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram |

Each option shows a count. **Clear** resets everything.

### Answer an ask

The card shows what happened, which Double is involved, where it came from, and how long it has been waiting. If several visitors asked the same thing, it shows **"Asked by N visitors"** and **"N waiting"**.

1. Click the primary action.
2. Review the context and any previous answers.
3. Write **Your answer**.
4. Choose **Send raw** or **Send framed** if Assist offers to adjust the wording. The **Visitor will see** preview shows the final message.
5. Click **Send**.

The answer goes to every visitor in the merged group. If delivery partially fails, the card tells you who was reached and lets you retry.

### Approve, fix, or decide

- **Approve** opens **Review and send**. Check the drafted message, then click **Send**.
- **Fix** usually takes you to the tool or channel that needs attention — for example, reconnecting Gmail.
- **Decide** resolves the item immediately.

A resolved item disappears from the queue, the header badge, the panel, and any open chat.

### Learning queue

If an answer was sent but saving it for future conversations failed, it appears under **Learning queue**. Click **Retry** to save it again.

### Recently handled

The last few resolved items are listed with **Handled** or **Skipped** and when they were resolved.

### Empty states

- Nothing open: **"You're all caught up"** — *"When a Double needs your OK, an answer or a fix, it shows up here."*
- Filters hide everything: **"Nothing of this type"** — *"Try another filter, or show all."*

## Activity

> Everything your Doubles and routines did, in plain words.

### Filters

- **All**
- **Calls**
- **Messages**
- **Routines**
- **Changes**
- **Waiting on you**

Pick one or more **Doubles**, or search by summary, note, or actor name. **Waiting on you** is the only filter with a count.

### Reading the log

Items are grouped by day: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date. The Today heading summarises the day:

> Today · 12 conversations · 3 leads · 5 tasks done

Each row shows the time, the actor, what happened, a short note, and an outcome:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Done** | Completed normally |
| **Booked** | A meeting or booking was created |
| **Sent** | A message or file was sent |
| **Needs you** | Waiting for your action |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong |
| **Undone** | Reverted from the log |

### Details and rollups

Click a row to open **What happened**, which shows the summary, the steps taken, the date and time, and a link to the conversation.

Rollups group repeated items — click **Show** to expand and **Hide** to collapse.

Some change entries show an **Undo** option in the row.

### Empty state

**"Nothing here yet"** — *"When your Doubles take calls, run routines or change settings, it shows up here."*

## What to know

- Resolving an ask anywhere clears it everywhere.
- Answers can reach multiple visitors at once when Assist merges identical questions.
- Activity records completed work; it is not a settings history.
- Assist shows these updates in-app only — there are no email or push notifications.
- A routine that needs attention appears in both **Needs you** and **Routines**.

## FAQ

**Why is an item in Needs you?** A Double needs your approval for a risky action, an answer it doesn't know, a fix for a disconnected tool, or a decision only you can make.

**Can I answer the same question for several visitors at once?** Yes. When Assist groups identical questions, one answer is sent to everyone in the group.

**I answered but the visitor didn't get it.** Open the item again and retry. Partial delivery is shown on the card.

**Does Activity include everything?** It includes calls, messages, routine runs, and changes made by your Doubles and routines.

**Can I undo a change from Activity?** Some entries show **Undo**. If there is no undo option, change it from the relevant settings page or ask Assist.
