title: "Routines"

Routines are jobs Assist runs for you on a schedule or when something happens — for example, *"every Friday, email me the top questions"* or *"when a new lead arrives, qualify them."*

You create them by describing them in chat. There is no routine form; Assist sets it up and asks before it goes live.

**Where to find it:** **Assist → Routines** (`/app/assist/routines`), or the **Routines** tab in the Assist panel.

## Create a routine

1. Open **Routines**.
2. Click **New routine** (or **Describe a routine** if you have none yet).
3. Assist opens with the composer pre-filled: `Set up a routine that `.
4. Finish the sentence — what should happen, who it is for, and when.
5. Send it. Assist builds the routine and asks for confirmation before it starts running.

**New routine** does not send anything on its own; it only puts the opening words in the composer.

### What a routine contains

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | A schedule, a one-time time, or an event such as a new email or a captured lead |
| **Do** | The instructions Assist follows |
| **Then** | Optional follow-ups, such as a reminder or a message |
| **Double** | The Double the routine works through |
| **Tools** | The apps and actions it may use |

Assist supports scheduled, one-time, event, app, and webhook triggers. The routine detail shows the trigger; only the schedule can be edited from the page.

## The Routines list

Use the filters to narrow the list:

- **All**
- **Needs attention**
- **Automations**
- **Scheduled**
- **One-time**

Each filter shows a count. **Search routines** matches the name, Double, status, and tool names.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The routine, with its kind: **Automation**, **Scheduled task**, or **One-time task** |
| **Doubles & resources** | The Double and tools it uses |
| **Runs** | How often it runs |
| **Last run** | When it last ran, or **Never** |
| **Next run** | When it runs next, **Paused**, or **—** |
| **Status** | **Active**, **Paused**, **Completed**, or **Needs attention** |

A blue dot labelled **New run result** means there is a fresh result to read. The row action is **Fix in chat** when the routine needs you, and **Open in chat** otherwise.

## Open a routine

Click a row to open its detail panel. It shows:

- The latest result and a link to any artifact it produced.
- **Open chat** and **Change in chat** — both prefill the composer so you can adjust it by describing the change.
- A plain-language **when / do / then** summary.
- **Trigger**, **Filters**, **Double**, **Last run**, and **Next run**.
- **How this works** — the prompt, the skill used, and the tools it needs.
- **Steps** — the ordered actions, with tags such as `writes` or `sends`.
- **Recent runs** — the last few results, marked **Ran** or **Failed**.
- **Action permissions**, **Follow-ups**, and **Memory**.

### Edit the prompt or schedule

Click **Edit prompt and schedule** (or the pencil) to open the editor:

- **Prompt** — what the routine should do.
- **Repeat** — **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
- **Every** — 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, or 1 month.
- **End repeat** — **Never** or **On date**.
- **Time** — the time of day, unless the routine is hourly.
- **Day** — the weekday, for weekly routines.
- **End date** — when **On date** is selected.

Click **Save**. The page confirms **"Routine updated."**

To change how a routine starts (for example, an event trigger), describe the change in chat.

### Pause, run, or delete

- **Pause** stops future runs; **Turn on** resumes them.
- **Run now** queues an immediate run. The result appears in **Activity**.
- **Delete routine** asks **"Delete this routine?"** and removes its schedule, memory, and follow-ups. Past runs stay in Activity.

**Run now** is unavailable while the routine needs attention, is already running, or has another blocking reason. The page tells you why.

## Permissions, memory, and follow-ups

### Action permissions

Some routines can make changes without asking. For each action you can choose:

- **Ask first** — the routine waits for your approval.
- **Allow without asking** — the routine can act on its own within limits.

Limits are shown per action: at most **1 allowed action per run**, **5 per day**, and at least **60 seconds apart**.

### Memory

Routines can remember useful context between runs — for example, who was already emailed. Memory is capped at five entries in the panel; use **Show all** to see them, **Forget** an entry, or **Forget everything**.

### Follow-ups

A routine can schedule a follow-up with an instruction and a time. The detail panel shows the follow-up or **Not scheduled**, and lets you cancel it.

### Google Chat replies

If a routine uses Google Chat, the panel shows whether replies are enabled. Click **Enable Google Chat reply** if it is off; sending still asks first.

## What to know

- Routines run across your workspace and can be scoped to one Double.
- Creating and changing triggers happens in chat; the page edits the prompt and schedule.
- A routine that needs you stays paused until the issue is fixed.
- If your monthly response limit is reached, a run is skipped with **"Skipped: the monthly response limit is reached."**
- Assist shows routine updates in-app only.

## Troubleshooting

**My routine didn't run.** Check its status — **Paused** and **Needs attention** routines do not run. Open it and use **Fix in chat** if it needs attention.

**Run now is greyed out.** The routine is already running, needs attention, or has a blocking reason shown under the buttons.

**I need a different schedule.** Use **Edit prompt and schedule**. For event or app triggers, ask Assist in chat.

**I deleted a routine — can I get it back?** No. Deleting removes the routine, its schedule, memory, and follow-ups. Its past runs remain in Activity.
