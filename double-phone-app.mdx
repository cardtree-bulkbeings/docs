---
title: "Double Phone App"
---

Your AI agent answers the calls you miss, screens unknown callers, follows rules you define, and can place calls on your behalf — speaking in your own voice.

Double Phone works on **+91 (Indian) numbers only**. You can set it up in the **Double Phone app** (Android and iPhone) or in the web app under **Channels → Phone**.

## App structure at a glance

| Tab | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Agent status (Active/Paused) · items needing your approval · recent calls |
| **Calls** | Filterable call list · search · call detail and transcript · caller details · corrections |
| **Rules** | Answer / Forward / Block / Silence rules · custom rule builder · known contacts · work hours · addresses |
| **You** | Voice setup · notifications · usage and plans · troubleshooting · help · log out |

During a live call, a mini call bar and a full-screen call view are available from any tab. In the web app, the same call list and settings live under **Channels → Phone**.

Call outcomes you will see: **Answered · Forwarded · Blocked · Silenced · Known contact (bypassed) · Not answered · Approval needed**.

## 1. Getting started

### Sign in

- **In the app:** continue with **Google** or **Apple**, or sign in with your email and password.
- **On the web:** continue with **LinkedIn** or **Google**, or use email and password (both required).

A successful sign-in takes you to Home.

### Verify your phone number

Enter your 10-digit Indian mobile number and request a code. Type the **6-digit code** from the SMS — the boxes advance as you type, and you can paste the whole code at once.

- **Resend / Send a new code** if it does not arrive.
- **Change number** to fix a typo.
- The number must be a 10-digit Indian mobile; `+91` is added for you.

### Set up your voice

Choose how your Double should sound:

- **Clone a new voice** — record a short sample, then pick a tone: **Warm**, **Friendly**, or **Formal**.
- **Use my Double voice** — reuses the voice from your existing Double account. During sign-up this stays locked until you sign in to that account; signing in pulls in your saved voice, tone and account knowledge.

Then pick at least one **spoken language** (for example English and Hindi) and tap **Continue**.

### You're all set

Review your tone and language chips. Keep **Call & message alerts** and **Sync my contacts** on for the full experience, then tap **Start using Double** to reach Home.

## 2. Connect and control your line

Open **Channels → Phone → Settings** in the web app, or the matching screen in the app.

### Connect your number

1. Enter **Your mobile number** (`+91`, 10 digits).
2. Tap **Connect number**. If the number needs confirming, a 6-digit code is texted to it.
3. Enter the code and tap **Confirm and connect**.

When the line is connected you will see:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Your number** | The mobile you connected |
| **Double Phone number** | The number your Double answers on |
| **Status** | **Answering** or **Paused** |

### Turn forwarding on from your phone

Your carrier still has to send calls to your Double. Dial these codes from the phone that owns the number, then press call:

| Situation | Code |
| --- | --- |
| When you don't answer | `**61*<Double Phone number>#` |
| When you're busy | `**67*<Double Phone number>#` |
| When your phone is off | `**62*<Double Phone number>#` |
| Turn forwarding off | `##004#` |

### Pause, resume, log out

- **Pause** stops your Double answering. Calls are **not** redirected to you while carrier forwarding is on — to get them back, turn forwarding off with `##004#`, then pause from the app or web.
- **Resume** starts answering again on the same number.
- **Log out** removes this Double's line from your account. It does **not** change the forwarding set on your phone, so dial `##004#` to stop calls going to the old number. You can reconnect the same number later.

## 3. Calls

### Call list

Filter with **All, Incoming, Outgoing, Missed**, and search by name, number, purpose or anything said in the transcript. Each row shows the caller, time, a preview line and a badge for live or urgent calls.

### What each outcome means

| Outcome | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Answered** | Your Double picked up and handled the call |
| **Forwarded** | The call rang through to you |
| **Blocked** | Your Double declined it |
| **Spam** | Declined automatically as spam or telemarketing |
| **Known contact** | A saved contact called, so your Double did not answer (also labelled _bypassed_) |
| **Unknown in work hours** | Not answered because of your work-hours setting |
| **Not answered** | It rang out |
| **Forwarding failed** | The call was forwarded but your Double could not pick up |
| **Not enough credits** | The call was refused; add credits in Billing |
| **Billing unavailable** | Phone billing was temporarily down; try again |

### Live call view

While a call is live you see a **LIVE** badge, a running timer, the caller's purpose, and a transcript that grows as the call happens.

- **Take over** — joins you to the call. After "Connecting…" the buttons become **Hand back to Double** and **End call**.
- **Chevron** — minimises to the mini bar; the rest of the app stays usable.
- **End call** — ends the call. In Assist, the same **Take over** and **End call** controls appear on the live call card.

### After the call

Calls that needed something from you show a summary card with the outcome and actions such as **Approve change** or **Suggest another**. Approving confirms in place. Calls that needed nothing show **Done**.

### Call detail

- Two-sided transcript (Double vs caller), with tags such as "Used: Home address" showing what informed a reply.
- **Call back** places an outbound call to the same number.
- **Correct or improve this answer** opens an editor prefilled from the call so you can teach your Double.
- **Caller details** shows call history, a **Block** toggle and **Add to contact**.

## 4. Place a call

Open **Calls → Call**, or use **Call back** on an existing call.

1. Pick a contact or type a number. Indian mobile or landline, 10 digits — `+91` is added for you.
2. Say **why** your Double is calling, in up to **120 characters**. This is the purpose it sticks to.
3. Press **Call now**. A **5-second countdown** starts; press **Cancel call** to stop it.
4. The call appears in the list as **Calling…**, then goes live when the other side picks up.

Your Double introduces itself as calling on your behalf, never shares OTPs or bank details, and ends the call once the purpose is done. The app allows **up to 20 outbound calls a day**, and the server rate-limits rapid repeat dialling.

## 5. Rules and approvals

### The main switches

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Block spam calls** | Declines calls that look like spam or telemarketing |
| **Let saved contacts ring through** | Known contacts ring you instead of your Double |
| **Answer unknown callers in work hours** | Off means unknown numbers are left unanswered during work hours |
| **Alert me about urgent calls** | Flags urgent calls so you can take over |

Changes save immediately. If the phone service refuses a change, the switch flips back and an error appears.

### Work hours and time zone

Work hours use your account time zone (default **Asia/Kolkata**). Outside them your Double says you are unavailable and offers to take a message.

### Addresses

Save up to **10 addresses** (for example home, for directions). An address is shared only when a live call asks for it and you **approve** it — a delivery agent asking for your home address triggers a share/decline card.

### Known, blocked and silenced numbers

- **Known contacts** always ring straight through; your Double never answers for them.
- **Blocked contacts** are declined. Add a number from caller details, or from the blocked list with an optional name.
- **Silenced contacts** are muted quietly — not rejected, not disconnected.

### Custom rules

Describe the rule in plain words in the rule builder ("If a delivery agent calls, take the details and ask me before sharing the gate code"). Double proposes a structured **When / Do** card you can **Edit** and **Save**.

- Up to **1,000 characters** per description.
- Drafting is rate-limited to about **10 rules a minute**.
- Drafting uses Double AI Credits, like other AI actions.

### Approvals

Some actions always need your decision: moving a meeting, sharing an address, or anything your rules mark as sensitive. The item appears on **Home** and in **Calls → Approvals**; **Approve** or **Deny** resolves it on the spot.

## 6. Greetings

The phone greeting is the first line callers hear. It is separate from your Double's chat greeting.

- Add up to **10 greetings**; each is capped at **160 characters**.
- `<name>` becomes the caller's name, or "there" when unknown.
- **Static** cycles through your greetings in order; **Dynamic** varies them.
- Leave the list empty to use your Double's own greeting.
- Tap **Save greeting** to apply. Changes are not live until saved.

## 7. Voice and languages

- **Voice Setup** shows your current voice and languages; the editor is the same one used at sign-up, saved with **Save voice**.
- **Your Double voice** becomes selectable once you are signed in to the account that owns it.
- Cloning needs a short, clean sample and your recorded consent. You can revoke a cloned voice later.
- Spoken languages decide what your Double can answer in; the caller can switch language mid-call.

## 8. Usage and billing

Double Phone calls are paid for with **Double AI Credits** — inbound and outbound calls have different per-minute rates, and the charge is recorded when the call ends.

- When your balance is empty, calls are refused with **"Not enough credits. Add credits in Billing."**
- If the billing service is briefly unavailable, the call shows **"Phone billing is temporarily unavailable. Please try again."**
- **Usage & Plans** (in the app) and **Settings → Limits & usage** (on the web) show the balance and the spend split by calls, chat and knowledge processing.
- The same credit wallet pays for calls, chat and knowledge processing; there is no separate phone balance.

## 9. Privacy and compliance

- Your Double **introduces itself as an assistant**. It does not pretend to be you.
- Calls can be recorded and transcribed so you can read them later. Recording is disclosed on the call and in the product.
- Recordings are never used to train models.
- OTPs, card numbers and bank details are blocked from being shared on a call.
- Voice cloning requires explicit consent and can be revoked.

## FAQ

**Why didn't my Double answer?** Check, in order: the line is not **Paused**; you have credits; the caller is not a known contact; **Answer unknown callers in work hours** is on; **Block spam calls** did not decline it. The call row shows the reason.

**What is the difference between pausing and logging out?** Pausing keeps the line connected and stops answering. Logging out removes the line from your account. Neither turns off carrier forwarding — dial `##004#` for that.

**What does "Known contact" or "Bypassed" mean?** The caller is one of your known contacts, so your Double deliberately did not pick up and the call rang you instead.

**How do I stop a number calling me?** Caller details → **Block** toggle, or **Rules → Blocked contacts → Block a number**.

**How do I fix a wrong answer?** Open the call → **Correct or improve this answer** → submit the correction.

**Can my Double call anyone?** It is built for callbacks and follow-ups, not cold dialling: Indian numbers only, a stated purpose, a daily cap and rate limits. It will not read out OTPs or bank details.

**Why did "Call now" not dial immediately?** There is a deliberate 5-second undo window. Wait for the countdown, or press **Cancel call** to stop it.

**Will logging out stop my Double?** Logging out disconnects this line. If forwarding is still set on your phone, calls keep going to the Double Phone number but will not be answered — dial `##004#` to send them back to your phone.

**My greeting was not used.** Save it with **Save greeting**, keep it under 160 characters, and make sure the greeting list is not empty. An empty list falls back to your Double's own greeting.
