Your AI agent answers, screens, forwards, or blocks incoming calls for you — speaking in your own voice and following rules you define.

## App structure at a glance

| Tab | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Agent status (Active/Paused) · items needing your approval · recent calls |
| **Calls** | Filterable call list · call details · caller details · corrections for your Double |
| **Rules** | Answer / Forward / Block / Silence rules · custom rule builder · known contacts, work hours, addresses |
| **You** | Voice setup · notifications · usage & plans · troubleshooting · help · log out |

During a live call, a mini call bar and a full-screen call view are available from any tab.

Call outcomes you will see across the app: **Answered · Forwarded · Blocked · Silenced · Bypassed** (a known contact called, so your Double did not pick up) **· Approval needed**.

## 1. Getting started

### Sign in

Open the app and sign in with **LinkedIn**, **Google**, or **email + password** (email and password are both required). A successful sign-in takes you to Home.

### Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number and tap **Send code**. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS — the boxes advance automatically as you type, and you can paste the whole code at once. Use **Resend code** if it does not arrive, or **Change number** to fix a typo.

### Tell Double your name

Enter your first and last name (at least one is required). Your Double uses these to introduce you on calls.

### Set up your voice

Choose how your Double should sound:

- **Clone a new voice** — record a short sample, then pick a tone: **Warm**, **Friendly**, or **Formal**.
- **Use my Double voice** — reuses the voice from your existing Double account. During sign-up this option is locked until you log in to that account; logging in pulls in your saved voice, tone, and account knowledge.

Then pick at least one **spoken language** (you can select several, e.g. English + Hindi) and tap **Continue**.

### You're all set

Review your tone and language chips. Keep **Call & message alerts** and **Sync my contacts** on for the full experience, then tap **Start using Double** to reach Home.

## 2. Home

Home shows a greeting, your Double's status card, anything waiting on you, and recent calls.

- **Status card** — shows today's counts (calls today, handled by Double, forwarded). The **Active/Paused** switch controls the agent: when paused, your Double stops picking up and every call rings straight through to you.
- **Attention cards** — items needing a decision, e.g. approving a meeting change or sharing your home address. **Approve** or **Deny** resolves the card on the spot.
- **Recent calls** — each row shows the caller, time, duration, and an outcome badge. Tap any card or row for full details.

## 3. Live calls

When a call comes in, your Double picks up and starts screening. A **mini bar** appears above the tab bar ("Double is picking up", then the caller's name with a live timer) — the rest of the app stays usable while it runs. Tap **View call** for the full-screen view.

### Full-screen call view

Shows a LIVE badge, timer, the caller's intent, and a **live transcript** that grows as the conversation happens. Some lines carry a rule tag (e.g. "Rule: changes need approval").

- **Take over** — joins you to the call ("Connecting…" first, then "You're on the call — Double stepped back"). The actions become **Hand back to Double** and **End call**.
- **Chevron** — minimises back to the mini bar.
- **End call** — ends the call.

### After the call

If the call needed something from you, a **summary card** appears with the outcome (e.g. "Meeting change requested") and actions such as **Approve change** or **Suggest another**. Approving confirms in place ("Approved · meeting moved to 4 PM"). Calls needing nothing show **Done**. Closing the view returns to Home, with resolved items removed.

## 4. Calls & approvals

### Calls list

Filter with the pills: **All, Approvals, Answered, Forwarded, Blocked, Silenced**. Each row shows caller, time, duration, a preview line, and an outcome badge.

### Call detail

- **Needs approval** — shows what happened, an approval card (**Approve / Suggest another**), and the two-sided transcript (Double vs Caller). Approving turns the card green with a confirmation.
- **Answered calls** — read-only detail. Lines that used stored knowledge carry a tag like "Used: Home address" so you can see what informed the reply.
- **⋯ menu** — opens **Caller details**.
- **Call back** and **Save contact** are available from the detail view.
- **Correct or improve this answer** — opens an editor prefilled from the call so you can feed a correction back to your Double.

### Caller details

Shows the caller header, call history, a **Block** toggle, and **Add to contacts**. Note: a contact-name match is not proof of identity — your Double treats callers by phone number. Turning Block ON labels the number "Blocked on Phone" and adds it to Blocked contacts.

## 5. Rules & call handling

The **Rules** tab groups everything by action:

- **Answer rules** — e.g. answer unknown calls in work hours, delivery-agent handling (address shared only after approval), urgent-call notifications, and locked protections like never sharing OTPs or bank statements.
- **Forward rules** — when calls ring straight through to you.
- **Blocking** — spam auto-block plus your **Blocked Contacts** list (remove any entry with ×, or add one via **Block a number**, which asks for an optional name and the number).
- **Silencing** — **Silenced Contacts** are muted quietly: not rejected, not disconnected.
- **General setup** — **Work Hours** (e.g. Mon–Sat, 9 AM–6 PM; outside these hours your Double says you're unavailable and offers to take a message) and **Addresses** (e.g. home for directions).
- **Known contacts** — numbers that always ring straight through; your Double never answers for them.
- **Add a custom rule** — describe the rule in plain words in the chat builder; Double proposes a structured rule card (**When / Do**) that you can **Edit** and **Save**.

## 6. You, settings & account

- **Voice Setup** — shows your current voice and languages; same editor as onboarding, saving with **Save voice**. Your Double voice becomes selectable once you're logged in.
- **Channels & Knowledge** — opens thedouble.ai for the wider product.
- **Notifications** — a feed of events across channels (calls, chat).
- **Usage & Plans** — your credit balance (e.g. 3,240 of 5,000 on Pro), split by calls, chat, and knowledge processing, plus the Free / Pro / Business plan list.
- **Troubleshooting** — permission toggles (phone, notifications, contacts) and phone checks (Carrier Check, Call Forwarding Check) with status badges.
- **Help Centre** — FAQs including how Double answers, rule priority, voice privacy, and contacting support.
- **Log out** — asks for confirmation first. Signing out clears the session on this device only; your Double keeps screening calls in the background.

## 7. More screens

- **Channels** — every place people can reach your Double. Active: Double Chat, Website, Card, Phone. Coming soon: WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, Email.
- **Shared knowledge** — add sources (upload documents, text, images) and browse your searchable knowledge base.
- **Phone & call handling** — health status, a test call, notification preview, and a setup summary (managed number, routing, status, voice, languages, schedule, blocked numbers, privacy) plus a do-not-share rule.
- **Notification settings** — toggles for call-start alerts, needs-attention alerts, call summaries, and caller details on the lock screen (off by default for privacy).
- **Call analytics** — weekly totals (e.g. 34 calls, 88% handled without you) split by outcome: answered, forwarded, declined as spam.

## FAQ

**How does my Double answer for me?** It picks up unknown calls, screens them in your voice, follows your rules, and only involves you when your input is needed (approvals) or your rules say to forward.

**What happens when I pause the agent?** All calls ring straight through to you until you switch it back on. Nothing else changes.

**What does "Bypassed" mean?** The caller is one of your known contacts, so your Double deliberately did not pick up.

**How do I stop a number calling me?** Caller details → Block toggle, or Rules → Blocked Contacts → Block a number.

**How do I fix a wrong answer my Double gave?** Open the call → Correct or improve this answer → submit the correction.

**Will logging out stop my Double?** No — it only signs out this device. Screening continues in the background.
