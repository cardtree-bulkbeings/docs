title: "Earnings and Payment History"
---

The **Earnings** page is where you track what your monetized Doubles have sold and what is waiting to reach your bank.

**Where to find it:** **Results → Earnings** for the whole account, or open a Double → **Results → Earnings** for that Double's sales.

## Summary cards

At the top of the page:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total earnings** | Revenue from all paid purchases |
| **Paid sessions** | Number of completed purchases |
| **Monetized Doubles** | Doubles with monetization turned on |
| **Total Doubles** | All Doubles on your account |

## Your Doubles

The **Your Doubles** table gives you a per-Double view:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Double** | The Double's link |
| **Monetization** | On/off switch for that Double |
| **Voice rate** | The effective rate per voice minute |
| **Paid sessions** | Completed purchases on that Double |
| **Total earned** | Revenue from that Double |
| **Action** | **Manage** opens the Double's settings |

You can turn monetization on or off for each Double here. Turning it on requires a verified payout account.

## Payment history

Below the table, **Payment history** lists every completed purchase and payment attempt for the Double you are viewing.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | The visitor's name and email, or **Anonymous visitor** |
| **Double** | Which Double the purchase was for |
| **Package** | Number of packages, plus the replies and voice time included |
| **Amount** | What the visitor paid |
| **Status** | **Successful**, **Pending**, **Failed**, or **Expired** |
| **Date** | When the purchase was made |
| **Details** | Opens the payment IDs |

Click **Refresh** to reload the list. Newest purchases appear first.

Open **Details** to see:

- **Razorpay Order ID**
- **Razorpay Payment ID**
- **Fulfillment** status
- **Purchase ID**

These IDs are useful when contacting support or matching a payment in Razorpay.

## How money reaches you

- Payments are collected by Razorpay.
- Your share is transferred automatically to your verified settlement account.
- Payouts are settled to your bank by Razorpay.
- Double takes **no commission**; the full payment amount is transferred to you.

The payout card at the top of Earnings shows the account status (**Ready**, **Under review**, **Action required**) and whether settlements are enabled. Use **Refresh status** to pull the latest state.

**Note:** Earnings shows account readiness and per-purchase records. Bank settlement statements are provided by Razorpay, not by the Earnings page.

## What to know

- Totals are gross purchase amounts; Razorpay's own processing fees are handled by Razorpay.
- **Pending** purchases are checkouts that have not been confirmed yet.
- **Failed** purchases were not charged successfully.
- **Expired** purchases were started but not completed in time.
- A purchase shows **Successful** when the payment is confirmed, even if the owner transfer is still being processed.
- Payment history only appears for a Double you have selected. Open that Double's **Earnings** page to see its sales.
- Credits are added to the visitor only after payment is verified.

## FAQ

**Why is my total lower than what visitors paid?** Totals are per currency and exclude nothing from your side, but Razorpay may deduct its processing fee before settling to your bank. Check your Razorpay settlement statement for the exact amount.

**Why do I see a payment with no customer name?** The visitor checked out as a guest and skipped the optional email step.

**Can I export payments?** Not from the Earnings page today. Use the Razorpay dashboard for accounting exports.

**A payment says Failed but the visitor was charged.** Contact support with the Razorpay Payment ID from **Details**.

**Why is a paid purchase not in my bank yet?** Transfers can take a little time after capture. Check the payout account status and the transfer in Razorpay.
