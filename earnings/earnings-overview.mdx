title: "Earnings Overview"
---

Earnings is where you charge visitors to talk to your Double. You set one credit package — a price, a number of **voice minutes**, and a number of **chat replies** — and visitors buy it before they continue a conversation.

Everything you earn goes to your own bank account through Razorpay. Double does not take a commission.

**Where to find it:** go to **Results → Earnings** in the sidebar (`/app/earnings`), or open a Double and choose **Results → Earnings** to see its sales.

## How it works

| Piece | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Account package** | One price and credit package shared by every Double you monetize |
| **Monetization switch** | A master switch for your account, plus an on/off switch for each Double |
| **Visitor purchase** | Visitors buy 1, 2, or 3 packages in a single checkout |
| **Credits** | Chat replies and voice minutes are tracked separately, and belong to that Double |
| **Payouts** | Each payment is transferred to your verified Razorpay settlement account |

### Credits

- **1 chat credit = 1 reply from your Double.** Visitor messages are free.
- **Voice credits are stored in seconds** and shown to visitors as minutes and seconds.
- Credits **never expire**.
- Credits are **per Double** — credits bought on one Double cannot be used on another.
- Buying again **adds to the balance**; it never replaces it.
- Chat and voice balances are separate. Spending voice minutes never uses chat replies.

## Before you can turn it on

1. **Set up payouts.** You need a verified Razorpay settlement account with payouts enabled. See **Setting Up Payouts**.
2. **Create your account package.** Set a price and at least one voice minute or chat reply.
3. **Publish the Double.** Only published Doubles can take payments.
4. **Turn on monetization** for your account, then for each Double.

Until payouts are ready, you can't turn monetization on for a Double; you'll be prompted to finish payout setup.

**Note:** Monetization is separate from **Double AI Credits**, which pay for your own workspace usage (chats, voice, tool actions, knowledge imports). Monetization is money coming *in* from visitors; Double AI Credits are what your workspace spends.

## What visitors see

- A **Buy credits / Buy replies / Buy voice** button in the chat header when a balance runs low.
- A paywall when they try to send a message or start a call without credits.
- A package picker with **1x**, **2x**, and **3x** quantity options, including any multi-package discount.
- An optional **Save your credits** step so guests can recover credits on another device.
- A payment screen powered by Razorpay (UPI, cards, and net banking).
- A success screen with their new balances.

Visitors can also recover previously purchased credits with their verified email.

## What you can see

**Earnings** shows:

- **Total earnings** — from all paid purchases
- **Paid sessions** — completed purchases
- **Monetized Doubles** — Doubles with monetization on
- **Total Doubles** — all Doubles on your account

Below that, **Your Doubles** shows each Double's monetization status, voice rate, paid sessions, and total earned. **Payment history** lists individual purchases with the customer, package, amount, status, and Razorpay IDs.

## What to know

- Prices are charged in **INR**. International payments in USD are coming soon.
- PayPal is not available yet.
- The same package applies to all your Doubles; you choose which Doubles are monetized.
- A Double must be published before visitors can buy credits on it.
- Payouts go to one bank account per owner, shared by all your Doubles.

## FAQ

**Do visitors get free messages before the paywall?** No. Once a Double is monetized, the first chat reply or voice call needs credits.

**Can I charge different prices on different Doubles?** Not today. The package is account-wide; monetization is switched on per Double.

**Do credits expire?** No.

**Can a visitor use credits on a different Double?** No. Credits are tied to the Double they were bought on.

**What happens if a reply fails?** The chat credit is returned automatically, so the visitor is not charged for an error.

**Where do refunds appear?** There is no self-serve refund flow. Contact support if a payment needs to be reviewed.

**Can I change the price after someone bought credits?** Yes. Existing balances are unaffected; the new price applies to future purchases.

**Do I need a separate Razorpay account?** No. Double creates and verifies your payout account with Razorpay for you.
