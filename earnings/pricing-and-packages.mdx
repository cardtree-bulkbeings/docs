---
title: "Pricing and Packages"
---

Monetization uses one credit package for your whole account. You choose the price, how many **voice minutes** and **chat replies** each package includes, and whether buying more than one package earns a discount.

**Where to find it:** **Results → Earnings → Account package**.

## Turn monetization on

At the top of **Earnings** there is a **Monetization** switch with **On** and **Off** states.

1. Turn **Enable Monetization** on.
2. Set up your package and save it.
3. Turn on individual Doubles in **Your Doubles**.

Both switches matter: the account switch turns monetization on for your workspace, and each Double has its own switch.

**Note:** The account switch does not check payout status by itself. You still cannot enable a Double until your payout account is ready.

## Set your package

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Package price** | What a visitor pays for one package, in INR |
| **Voice call** | Voice minutes included in each package |
| **Chat** | Chat replies included in each package |

The package summary updates as you type, for example:

> Each **₹200** package includes **20** voice minutes and **60** chat replies.

Click **Save changes** to apply the package to all your Doubles.

### Price rules

- The price must be greater than **0**.
- Prices are entered in whole rupees — no decimals.
- Currency is **INR (₹)**. International payments in USD are coming soon.
- At least one of voice minutes or chat replies must be greater than 0.
- Visitors see the price per package and the total for the quantity they choose.

## Multi-package discounts

Turn on **Multi-package discounts** to reward visitors who buy more than one package.

| Quantity | Default discount |
| --- | --- |
| **2 packages** | 5% |
| **3+ packages** | 10% |

- The discount applies to the package subtotal.
- Only the **highest eligible tier** applies — discounts do not stack. Buying 3 packages with a 5% and a 10% tier gets 10%.
- The payment screen shows the package subtotal, the discount, and the final amount.

## Payment methods

Under the package settings, the **Payment methods** card shows how you get paid.

- **UPI via Razorpay** — UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking. Shows **Accepting payments: Active** when the account is set up, and **Receive earnings: Connected (•••• 1234)** or **Not connected**.
- **PayPal** — international payments. Coming soon and disabled.

If payouts are not connected, click **Connect payout account** to open the payout setup flow.

## Enable monetization for each Double

The **Your Doubles** table lists every Double with:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Double** | The Double's public link |
| **Monetization** | On/off switch for that Double |
| **Voice rate** | The effective per-minute rate |
| **Paid sessions** | Completed purchases |
| **Total earned** | Revenue from that Double |
| **Action** | **Manage** opens the Double's lead and payment settings |

Turning a Double on requires a ready payout account. If it isn't ready, you'll see:

> Connect and verify your Razorpay payout account before enabling monetization.

## What to know

- One package applies to all Doubles. You control which Doubles are monetized, not their individual prices.
- A Double must be published to take payments.
- Saving a package while monetization is off is allowed; you just can't enable a Double until the price and credits are valid.
- Changing the price never changes credits visitors already bought.
- Visitors can buy 1, 2, or 3 packages per checkout.

## Troubleshooting

**"Please enter a valid package price greater than 0."** Enter a whole number above zero.

**"Please specify at least 1 voice minute or 1 chat reply in the package."** A package needs at least one kind of credit.

**"Monetization currently supports INR (₹)."** Change the currency to INR. The current UI only supports INR.

**The Double switch is disabled.** Finish payout setup, then try again.

**The discount isn't adding up.** Only the highest tier for the chosen quantity applies; tiers do not combine.
