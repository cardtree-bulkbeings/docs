---
title: "Setting Up Payouts"
---

Before you can charge visitors, you need a payout account. Double creates it with **Razorpay Route** using your details, Razorpay verifies it, and then every payment is transferred straight to your bank.

There is no separate Razorpay signup, and Double takes no commission — the full payment amount is transferred to you.

**Where to find it:** **Results → Earnings → Monetization & payment account**.

## Connect your payout account

1. Open **Results → Earnings**.
2. Under **Monetization & payment account**, click **Set up payments** (or **Complete payout setup** if you already authorized a Razorpay account).
3. Complete the three steps.
4. Click **Submit details**.

Razorpay verifies your identity and bank account before payouts can start. You can keep using Double while you wait; the payout card shows the current status.

### Step 1 — Your details

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Full legal name** | The name on your bank account and PAN |
| **Email address** | Where Razorpay can reach you about the account |
| **Mobile number** | A 10-digit Indian mobile number |

If you connected a Razorpay account, it appears here as **Connected Razorpay Account** with an **Authorized** badge.

### Step 2 — Bank details

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Account holder name** | The name on the bank account |
| **Account number** | 5–20 digits |
| **Confirm account number** | Must match exactly |
| **IFSC code** | 11 characters, for example `HDFC0000317` |
| **PAN (optional)** | 10 characters, for example `ABCDE1234F` |

### Step 3 — Review and submit

Check the account holder, bank destination, IFSC, and connected account or PAN. Then tick:

> I agree to the Razorpay Terms of Service and authorize Double to direct my customer earnings to this verified settlement account.

**Submit details** stays disabled until the box is ticked.

## Payout statuses

| Status | What it means | Can you enable monetization? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Not configured** | No payout account yet | No |
| **OAuth Connected** | Razorpay authorized, but Route payout setup is incomplete | No |
| **Pending verification** | Account linked; waiting for Razorpay to verify and activate settlements | No |
| **Under review** | Details submitted and being checked | No |
| **Ready** | Account is active and verified to receive automatic payouts | **Yes** |
| **Action required** | Razorpay needs details corrected, or the account is suspended | No |
| **Setup unavailable** | The account was rejected | No |

When the status is **Ready**, the card shows **Ready for monetization**, **Settlements enabled: Active**, and **Monetization readiness: Ready**.

## Checking and fixing your status

- Click **Refresh status** to fetch the latest state from Razorpay.
- If Razorpay needs corrections, the card lists exactly what is missing or needs fixing. Click **Resolve now** or **Update details** to reopen the form.
- If your account is rejected or suspended, contact support.
- Double checks your status after you submit and notifies you in the app when verification finishes:
  - **"Your payment account is verified and ready for payouts!"**
  - **"Bank verification requires attention. Please check your bank details."**

## What to know

- One payout account covers **all** your Doubles. You cannot route different Doubles to different bank accounts.
- Your full PAN and account number are sent to Razorpay, not stored by Double. Only the last four digits are shown back to you.
- Payout setup is separate from your Double AI Credits billing. This account only receives money from visitor purchases.
- If payouts are not ready, monetization switches stay off and visitors cannot be charged.
- If a payout account becomes inactive later, visitors can still receive the credits they paid for, but the transfer to you is marked failed until the account is restored.

## Troubleshooting

**The switch won't turn on.** Finish payout setup first. Double requires an active, verified settlement account with payouts enabled.

**My details were rejected.** Open **Earnings → Monetization & payment account**, read the requirements list, and click **Resolve now** or **Update details**.

**Verification is taking longer than expected.** Click **Refresh status**. If it is still under review after a few business days, contact Razorpay support.

**I changed banks.** Use **Update details** from the payout card and submit the new account. Razorpay verifies the new account before payouts move to it.

**I see "Another workspace" or an authorization error.** Reconnect Razorpay from **Earnings**; the authorization window must be completed in the same browser session.
