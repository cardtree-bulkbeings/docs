---
title: "After the Meeting"
---

Go to the **Activity** tab to see your past meetings. Search by title, meeting code, or name, and sort by newest, oldest, or longest.

## **What you can do**

Open a meeting to:

- Read and search the **Transcript**
- Click **Generate recap** for a **Recap**, **Key points**, and **Follow-ups**
- Click **Download transcript**

**Note:** The transcript can take a minute or two to become available after the call closes.
