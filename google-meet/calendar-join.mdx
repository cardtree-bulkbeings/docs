---
title: "Calendar Join"
---

Let your Double join eligible Google Meet events automatically.

## **How to turn on Calendar Join**

1. Go to **Your Double → Channels → Google Meet**, on the **Calendar** tab
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** and sign in
3. Choose a **Calendar**
4. Choose a **Join lead time**: at the start time, or 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes before
5. Turn on **Calendar Join**

## **Which events are joined**

- Events with a Google Meet link and a real start time, in the next 24 hours
- Declined, cancelled, all-day, and Meet-less events are skipped

## **Managing upcoming meetings**

The **Upcoming & recent** list shows each meeting and its status:

| **Status** | **What it means** |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Your Double will join at the lead time |
| **Joining** | Your Double is on its way in |
| **Waiting for admission** | Someone in the meeting needs to admit it |
| **In meeting** | Your Double is in the call |
| **Completed** | The meeting has ended |
| **Skipped** | Your Double won't join, or didn't |
| **Failed** | The join didn't work. The row shows the reason |

- Click **Skip** on a scheduled meeting to keep your Double out of it
- Click **Check now** to look for new meetings straight away

## **What to know**

- Calendar Join only reads your calendar. It never posts to Google Calendar
- The bot joins as an AI assistant and leaves after four hours at the latest
- Changing the calendar or Google account turns Calendar Join off until you turn it back on
- If Google stops accepting the saved access, click **Reconnect** to turn it back on
- Calendar meetings use credits and voice minutes, just like a manual join
- Calendar meetings use the opening instruction and participant name saved for this Double
