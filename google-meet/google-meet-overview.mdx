title: "Google Meet Overview"
---

Send your Double into a live Google Meet call. It hears the room, answers out loud in its own voice, and knows it is a guest in a meeting. Afterwards, you get the transcript and a recap.

Go to **Your Double → Channels → Google Meet** to get started. If the channel is off, click **Turn on** first.

### **What your Double can do in a meeting**

- Join from a link you paste, or automatically from your calendar
- Listen and answer out loud, using its knowledge, Persona, and voice
- Stay quiet until someone says its name, if you prefer
- Present a published deck on request
- Post a shareable file as a link in the meeting chat
- Give you a transcript and a recap after the call

### **The Google Meet tabs**

| **Tab** | **What it's for** |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Join a meeting now, follow the live transcript, and see what happened during a join |
| **Calendar** | Connect Google Calendar so your Double joins meetings automatically |
| **Activity** | Past meetings, transcripts, and recaps |
| **Settings** | How your Double behaves in the room |

### **Credits**

Time in a meeting uses credits and voice minutes. See [pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing) for current rates. Google Meet is available on every plan.
