---
title: "Joining a Meeting"
---

Send your Double into a Google Meet call straight away, or ask Assist to do it for you.

## **Join a meeting now**

1. Go to **Your Double → Channels → Google Meet**, on the **Overview** tab
2. Paste the Google Meet link into **Meeting link**
3. Review **Opening instruction**, **Name in the participant list**, and **When should it speak?**
4. Click **Join now**
5. In your meeting, admit your Double when it knocks

Click **Leave the meeting** at any time to remove it. The live transcript appears on the right while it is in the call.

## **What to know**

- Your Double has to be admitted like any guest. If nobody admits it in time, it doesn't join and you can send it again
- It leaves on its own shortly after everyone else has left
- The opening instruction and participant name are saved for this Double, so calendar meetings use them too
- Clear the opening instruction if you don't want it to greet the room

## **Join from Assist**

You can also ask Assist to do it for you, for example: "Join this Google Meet and send me the notes."

Assist can also schedule a join for later, up to 7 days ahead. For recurring meetings, use Calendar Join instead.
