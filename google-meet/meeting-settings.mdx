---
title: "Meeting Settings"
---

Set how your Double behaves in the room on the **Settings** tab. These go into its instructions for the call, so they change what it actually does.

## **When should it speak?**

| **Mode** | **What it does** |
| --- | --- |
| **When addressed** | Answers when someone puts a question to the meeting, and stays quiet through side talk |
| **Only when called** | Stays silent until someone says one of its trigger words |

Changes apply from the next time it joins.

## **Trigger words**

Names and phrases that get its attention, such as "Hey Double". You can add up to 8.

**Note:** In **Only when called** mode with no trigger words, your Double answers as it would in **When addressed**.

## **Standing instructions**

Applied to every meeting, on top of your Double's Persona (up to 600 characters).

**Example:** "Keep answers brief and practical. Ask before making commitments on my behalf."

## **Default opening line**

The first thing it does once admitted, used for every meeting including calendar joins (up to 400 characters). Clear it if you don't want it to greet the room.

## **Voice**

Your Double uses the same voice here as everywhere else. Change it from **Your Double → Voice**.
