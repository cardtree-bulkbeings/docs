## **Presenting a deck**

Ask your Double to present in the meeting, and it shares any presentation you have published for it. It shows the slides and talks through them, and you can ask it to move between slides or close them.

See Creating a Presentation to set up and publish a deck.

## **Sharing files in the meeting chat**

When someone asks for a file your Double is allowed to share, it posts a link in the meeting chat.

**What to know**

- Only shareable PDFs and images in your Knowledge can be sent
- **A shared link works for an hour, and anyone who has it can open it**
- A meeting can have up to 10 file links
- If a link can't be posted, your Double says so instead of claiming it is in the chat

See Sharing Files with Visitors to choose which files can be shared.
