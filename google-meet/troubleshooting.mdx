---
title: "Troubleshooting"
---

**Your Double can't join**

- Check that the link is a Google Meet link, such as `https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij`
- Make sure someone in the meeting is there to admit it
- If a calendar meeting shows **Failed**, its row shows the reason

**A calendar meeting isn't listed**

- Check that the event has a Google Meet link and starts within the next 24 hours
- Check that you haven't declined it
- Click **Check now**

**Calendar Join stopped working**

- If you changed the calendar or Google account, turn **Calendar Join** back on
- If Google stopped accepting the saved access, click **Reconnect**

**It talks too much, or not enough**

- Switch **When should it speak?** between **When addressed** and **Only when called**
- Add guidance to **Standing instructions**

**Reporting a problem**

The **What happened** panel on the **Overview** tab lists every step of a join. Click **Copy** and paste it into your support request.
