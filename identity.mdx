**Identity** is the profile people see wherever they meet your Double — in conversations, on your public page, and across channels. Find it in the per-Double sidebar under **Configure → Identity**. Everything saves automatically as you type (no Save button).

## Essentials — the core facts

- **Profile photo** — Change photo (image files only) or Remove. Shown in conversations and supported channels.
- **Name** — your display name (also used to greet visitors).
- **Job title** — e.g. Founder, Designer.
- **Company** — start typing (2+ letters) to search brands with logos, pick one, or "Use as entered". Upload or remove the company logo from the logo menu.
- **Location (optional)** — search a city (3+ letters) and **pick it from the list** so maps and time zones stay accurate.
- **About / bio** — a short description (3 rows).
- **More details** — optional **pronouns** (e.g. he/him).

## Contact and links

Fixed rows for **Website, Email, and Phone**, plus an **Add link** flow for social profiles: pick the kind (LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, Website, custom…), paste the URL, Add. Custom links can be relabelled or deleted with the trash icon. Links with empty URLs are ignored.

## Voice (right rail)

The **default voice** tied to this identity, split into your cloned and preset voices — listen to previews, Select one, or open full **Voice settings**. Voice changes save immediately and separately.

## Used in — where your identity appears

A read-only list of every surface inheriting this profile (Double Chat header, Card hero, Website widget intro, Gmail name and signature…). A channel may present it differently, but the source stays the same. Toggling channels on/off happens in the Channels hub, not here.

## Handle (your Double's link)

Your public link (`thedouble.ai/yourhandle`) is edited via the **pencil next to the handle** in the sidebar header, not on this page. Rules: 3–30 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only; availability is checked live, and changing it moves you to the same page under the new link.

## Saving & limits

- Text fields trim extra spaces on save; a failed save shows "Failed to save identity" without losing your edits.
- Photos upload straight to storage with an instant preview (reverted if the upload fails). There is no crop step.
- Company search needs 2+ characters, location 3+.

## FAQ

**Where do I change my Double's URL?** Sidebar header → pencil icon next to the handle.

**Why isn't my new photo showing somewhere?** It saves instantly to the shared profile; refresh the surface. Some channels cache images briefly.

**Do links I add show publicly?** Yes — platform and custom links with URLs appear wherever that surface shows links.

**Can I use different names per channel?** No — one shared identity feeds all surfaces by design. Presentation may differ per channel.

**I broke something — is my text lost?** No. Edits park safely on failed saves and retry; toasts confirm photo, logo, voice, and link actions.
