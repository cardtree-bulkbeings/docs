Knowledge is your AI Double's brain. Add files, websites, apps, and social content so your Double can answer accurately about you and your work, across every channel it runs on.

## **Why Knowledge matters**

A strong Knowledge library makes your AI Double more effective:

- Answers questions about your expertise
- Explains your services clearly
- Qualifies leads accurately
- Performs consistently across chat, voice, and meetings

Better knowledge = better conversations.

## **Where to find it**

- **Knowledge** in the main sidebar is your full library, across all Doubles
- **Your Double → Knowledge** shows what is training that Double, and lets you add more

## **What you can add**

Train your AI Double with multiple source types:

- **Documents** - PDF, Word, CSV, and text files
- **Custom text** - Information you write directly
- **Images** - Charts, diagrams, and visual content
- **Websites** - Your site, blog, or public pages
- **Google Drive** - Docs, Sheets, and files from your Drive
- **LLM Knowledge** - Background context for your AI
- **Substack** - Posts and articles from a publication
- **LinkedIn** - Profile, company page, or posts
- **X / Twitter** - Posts and replies
- **Instagram** - Posts authored by the account
- **TikTok** - Videos, captions, and spoken content
- **YouTube** - Video transcripts
- **Notion (Soon)** - Pages and databases from a workspace

Your AI Double combines all sources for complete answers.

## **Sharing files with visitors**

Your Double can also hand over files from your Knowledge, such as a brochure or price list, when you allow it.

## **LLM Knowledge**

Provide details about your work, expertise, and focus areas so your AI Double has full context on who you are and what you do.
