---
title: "Anonymous Threshold"
---

Choose how long a visitor can chat before they are asked for their details.

Go to **Your Double → Lead capture** and set the **Anonymous threshold**.

| **Setting** | **What it does** | **Default** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Messages** | The number of text messages a visitor can send | 5 |
| **Minutes** and **Seconds** | How long a visitor can talk in a voice conversation | 1 minute |

**Tip:** A low threshold captures more leads, but gives visitors less time to see value. Start with the defaults and adjust from there.
