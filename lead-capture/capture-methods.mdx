title: "Capture Methods"
---

Choose how visitors identify themselves. Go to **Your Double → Lead capture** and pick a **Capture method**.

## **Verified**

Visitors see **Verify to keep chatting**, and sign in with one click. You always get a confirmed identity.

- **LinkedIn:** Continue with LinkedIn
- **Gmail:** Continue with Google

Turn each option on or off. At least one has to stay on.

**Best for:** Getting reliable contact details with the least effort for the visitor.

## **Custom form**

Visitors fill in a form. Choose which fields to ask for:

| **Field** | **Required** |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | Always |
| **Email** | Always |
| **Job title** | Optional |
| **Phone number** | Optional |
| **Company name** | Optional |
| **Industry** | Optional |

Tick **Verify** next to **Email** to confirm the address is real. Visitors are sent a 6-digit code, and enter it to continue. They can ask for a new code if it doesn't arrive.

**Best for:** Collecting specific details, like company or job title.
