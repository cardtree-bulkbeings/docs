title: "Contacts"
---

Everyone you capture appears in **Contacts**. Search by name, email, or company.

## **Viewing a contact**

Open a contact to see:

- **Overview:** Lead information, including name, title, email, phone, company, and intent score
- **Timeline:** Their activity with your Double
- **Notes** and **Tags:** Your own notes and labels

You can edit a contact's details, or delete it.

## **Acting on new leads**

In **Assist → Routines**, you can have a routine run when **a new lead is captured**. It can use the details you collected, such as name, email, phone, and company, to take your next step.
