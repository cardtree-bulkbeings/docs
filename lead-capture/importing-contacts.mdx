Bring in contacts you already have.

## **How to import contacts**

1. Go to **Contacts**
2. Click **Import contacts**
3. Choose a source:
   - **Google Contacts and Gmail:** Import saved Google Contacts and Gmail
   - **CSV or Excel:** Upload a .csv, .xlsx, or .xls file
   - **iPhone or Android:** Choose contacts on supported phones, or upload a .vcf file
   - **LinkedIn:** Upload the Connections.csv file exported from LinkedIn

Sample files are available in the dialog if you need an example format.
