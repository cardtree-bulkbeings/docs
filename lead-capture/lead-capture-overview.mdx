---
title: "Lead Capture Overview"
---

Lead capture collects visitor contact details during a conversation. After a set number of messages or minutes, your Double asks the visitor to identify themselves before the conversation continues. Their details are saved to your **Contacts**.

Lead capture is set up for each Double, and is available on every plan.

### **How to turn on Lead capture**

1. Go to **Your Double → Lead capture**
2. Turn on **Lead capture**
3. Set your **Anonymous threshold**
4. Choose a **Capture method**

Changes save automatically. You can also get there from **Contacts**, by clicking **Lead capture**.

### **What the visitor sees**

1. The visitor chats with your Double as normal
2. When they reach the threshold, they see the sign-in options or the form
3. Once they complete it, the conversation continues

### **Where it works**

- Lead capture works on your Double's web conversations, in text and voice: your Double page, your Card, and your website agent
- It doesn't run on channels like Google Chat and WhatsApp
- Visitors who are already signed in aren't asked
- If Monetization is on for your Double, visitors share their details through the paywall flow instead

### **Best practices**

- Let visitors see value first. Give them a few messages before asking
- Use **Verified** for the fastest experience
- Only ask for fields you will use. Shorter forms get finished more often
- Turn on **Verify** for email if you plan to follow up by email
