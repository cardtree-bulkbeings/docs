---
title: "Troubleshooting"
---

**The prompt isn't appearing**

- Check that **Lead capture** is on for this Double
- Check that the visitor has reached the **Messages** or voice time threshold
- Test in a private or incognito window. Signed-in visitors, including you, aren't asked
- Lead capture doesn't run on Google Chat or WhatsApp
- If Monetization is on, visitors share their details through the paywall instead

**A visitor didn't get their verification code**

- Ask them to check their spam folder
- Ask them to resend the code, and to check the email address

**A contact isn't showing**

- Refresh **Contacts** and search by email
- Check that the visitor finished the sign-in or the form
