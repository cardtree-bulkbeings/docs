---
title: "Persona: Tone, Instructions & Behaviour"
---

Persona decides how your Double comes across — its tone, its manners, what it says first, how strictly it sticks to topics, and what it does when it doesn't know something. Together with **Voice** (how it sounds) and **Knowledge** (what it knows), Persona forms your Double's **Brain**.

Go to your **Double → Configure → Persona** to get started (the address may also appear as Personality — both open the same page). Everything saves automatically.

**What you can customize**

- Tone preset & characteristics
- Custom instructions
- Guardrails
- Greeting message
- Response structure
- Topic flexibility
- Auto-suggested replies
- Behavior when Double doesn't know something
- Style memory

## **Tone Preset**

Choose how your Double comes across in conversations.

Start with one of five presets:

- **Balanced**: Versatile and professional for most use cases
- **Friendly**: Warm, conversational, and approachable
- **Warm**: Supportive and empathetic
- **Professional**: Polished, clear, and confident
- **Quirky**: Creative, playful, and expressive

You can further fine-tune the preset under **Characteristics** using four sliders:

- **Enthusiasm:** Calm → Enthusiastic
- **Curiosity:** Direct → Inquisitive
- **Emoji usage:** None → Frequent (voice calls never use emojis)
- **Sentence length:** Concise → Detailed

Selecting a preset sets the starting values for these characteristics. Moving a slider marks your setup as a **Custom configuration** — you can **Reset variables to the preset** at any time to go back.

## **Custom Instructions**

Set permanent rules for how your Double behaves across every conversation.

Type your instructions in the text box — e.g. "Be very formal", "Use simple analogies", "Keep answers concise but friendly." You have up to 4000 characters (the counter shows usage; anything beyond may be ignored).

**Tip:** These instructions are added to every prompt. Use this to set permanent rules for your AI's behavior.

**How to write good custom instructions**

Be specific and direct. A few examples:

- "Always respond in bullet points when listing more than 3 items"
- "Never discuss competitors by name"
- "Always end with a question to keep the conversation going"

Custom instructions take priority over tone presets — if there's a conflict, instructions win.

## **Guardrails**

Rules your Double **must follow on every response and tool call** — e.g. "never share prices without confirming". Add them as chips via **Add guardrail**. Think of these as non-negotiable, while custom instructions are guidance.

## **Greeting**

Set the very first message a visitor sees when they open your chat.

- **Static** — one fixed opener, or a rotating carousel of up to 10 greetings (add with +, remove with ×, minimum one kept)
- **Dynamic** — generated fresh for each visitor
- Use `<name>` to insert the visitor's name (falls back to "there") — e.g. "Hello! How can I help you today?"

**Tip:** Keep it short and inviting. A good greeting sets the tone and signals to visitors what they can ask.

## **Response Structure**

**Person / Point of view** — choose how your Double refers to itself:

- **First person** — "I believe this approach will work well for your project"
- **Third person** — your Double refers to you by name or role (e.g. "Faraz believes this approach will work well for your project")

A live example on the page shows the difference. First person feels more natural for most use cases. Use third person if you want your Double to feel more like a dedicated assistant speaking on your behalf.

## **Topic Flexibility**

Topic Flexibility controls how your Double handles questions outside its core area of expertise.

Choose one of three levels:

- **Strict:** Stays within the profile's documented core scope and redirects unrelated questions.
- **Balanced:** (default) Handles core and directly related topics while staying grounded in the represented role.
- **Flexible:** Can discuss broader topics from the profile's perspective, while still maintaining its represented role and purpose.

For specialized or professional Doubles, **Strict** or **Balanced** is usually the better choice. Use **Flexible** when you want your Double to engage naturally across a wider range of conversations.

## **Auto Suggested Replies**

Toggle this on to automatically surface reply options for visitors during a conversation — keeping the conversation flowing without them having to type from scratch. Off by default.

Suggested replies appear as clickable prompts after your Double responds, generated dynamically based on the conversation context.

**Tip:** Keep this on for most use cases. It reduces friction for visitors and leads to longer, more productive conversations.

## **When Double Doesn't Know Something**

Set how your Double responds when a visitor asks something outside its knowledge.

- **Honest admission** (default) — "That's outside my expertise, but I'd be happy to help with related topics"
- Or describe your own preferred fallback response in the text box

**How to write a good fallback**

A good fallback acknowledges the limit, stays on-brand, and redirects the visitor. For example:

- "I don't have that information right now, but feel free to reach out to me directly at \[email\]"
- "That's a bit outside my wheelhouse — but happy to help with anything related to \[your expertise\]"

Avoid vague fallbacks like "I don't know." They leave visitors with nowhere to go.

## **Style Memory**

Your Double learns how you write from real conversations. **Draft** learnings await your approval; **approved** ones apply across all channels. This is separate from presets and instructions.

## **Behaviours vs Skills**

Use cases you pick at onboarding appear as "N skills · N behaviours":

- **Behaviours** — written into the instructions (e.g. Capture leads, Take messages)
- **Skills** — real capabilities created at launch (e.g. Present a deck, Book meetings, Share files). Some need their connected service or content configured after launch and show **"needs setup after launch"** until then — they won't run before that

**What to know**

- Persona settings apply to the specific Double shown — if you have multiple Doubles, configure Persona separately for each.
- Persona works alongside Knowledge Base and Voice — tone and behavior are set here, what your Double knows comes from knowledge sources, and how it sounds from voice settings.
- Custom instructions override preset behavior when there's a conflict.
