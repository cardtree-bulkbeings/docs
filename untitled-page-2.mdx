Double runs on **Double AI Credits** — one universal balance that pays for everything your Double does: answering chats, voice calls, running tool actions, and importing knowledge.

## Where to see your balance & usage

- **Avatar menu** — the meter shows credits used vs included this month, plus any top-up credits. It warns you at 80% used and at 100% shows Upgrade. Click it to open full details.
- **Settings → Limits & usage** — your **Current plan**, a **Double AI Credits** card (Monthly Credits, Top-up Credits, Doubles, Voice clones), a **Buy credits** button, and your Knowledge Base word usage.
- **Credit spend breakdown** — a chart splitting Total Spent by chat, voice, tool actions, and knowledge imports.
- **Recent credit activity** — a turn-by-turn ledger: date, activity, amount (−used / +added), balance after, and status (Completed, Stopped by user, Waived if our side errored — errors are never charged).
- **Usage alerts** — turn on in Notification settings to be warned when your balance drops below 20%.

Monthly credits reset each billing cycle. **Top-up credits never expire** and are used after monthly credits.

## What uses credits

- **Chat answers** — each reply, based on the AI model used
- **Voice** — calls and meetings, per second/minute
- **Tool actions** — e.g. booking, emails, CRM updates your Double performs
- **Knowledge imports** — adding websites, files, YouTube, LinkedIn, or social sources
- **Visitors** — when someone chats or calls your Double, it spends _your_ credits; if they run out mid-conversation, the visitor sees a paywall and you get a prompt to top up

## Buying credits (top-ups)

1. Go to **Settings → Billing** (or click **Buy credits** on the Limits & usage page).
2. Pick a pack (e.g. from \$10 = 10,000 credits — top-ups start around 10,000 credits and **never expire**).
3. Complete payment in the secure checkout (card, UPI, and other local methods via Dodo Payments).

Credits land in your wallet immediately after successful payment. Teams: only owners/admins can buy organisation top-ups. The Double team can also configure a **custom plan** for you, which you review and purchase the same way.

## Managing your subscription

**Settings → Billing** shows your current plan, billing cycle, price, next billing date, and payment method (card/UPI, masked). From here you can:

- **Upgrade plan** — opens the pricing page
- **Add top-ups** / view **Top-up history** (with CSV export)
- **Update payment method** — via the payment portal
- **Invoices** — the Billing/Invoice tab lists every charge with date, plan, status (Paid/Failed/Refunded), and a full receipt drawer
- **Cancel subscription** — cancels renewals; your plan stays valid till the end date shown, and you can restore before data is removed. Failed payments show a **Pay Invoice** option during the grace period

Questions about a charge: write to `hello@thedouble.ai`.

## When credits run out

Your Double pauses paid actions and you'll see prompts like **Buy Credits** (chats) or a voice-limit notice. Top up to resume instantly — monthly credits refill automatically on your next billing date.

## FAQ

**Do unused monthly credits roll over?** Lite rolls over up to 2 months. Top-up credits never expire on any plan.

**Why did a visitor see a paywall on my Double?** Their session spends your credits. Top up (or upgrade for a bigger monthly allowance) to keep visitor conversations running.

**I was charged for an error — what now?** System-side failures are automatically marked Waived (0 charge) in your activity ledger. If something looks wrong, contact `hello@thedouble.ai`.

**Can I get a refund on a top-up?** Refunds show in Top-up history as Partially/Refunded with amounts. Contact support for refund requests.

**How do I stop monthly billing but keep my data?** Cancel; everything works till the access date shown. Re-subscribe within the valid period to restore without data loss.
