---
title: "Choosing a Preset Voice"
---

Select a preset voice if you prefer not to clone or design your own. HQ and Lite presets are available.

**How to choose a preset voice**

1. Go to **Your Double → Voice**
2. Scroll to the **Voice library**
3. Click **Filters** to narrow by accent, gender, and voice type
4. Click **Play** next to any voice to preview it
5. Select the voice to apply it

**Preset voices**

| **Type** | **Voices** |
| --- | --- |
| **HQ** | Sakshi, Vijay, Linda, Thomas, Ava, Chloe, Grace, Ethan, Noah, Mason |
| **Lite** | Heart, Bella, Sky, Alice, Sarah, Adam, Daniel, Michael, Emma, George |

**What to know**

- Voices are marked with an **HQ** or **Lite** badge
- Presets use their built-in model. Lite is faster, HQ sounds more natural
- You can switch to a cloned or designed voice anytime
