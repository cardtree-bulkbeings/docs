---
title: "Cloning your voice"
---

Create a voice clone so your AI Double sounds like you. You only need a short, clear recording of your natural speaking voice.

## Requirements

- Length: **6 to 30 seconds**. **20 to 30 seconds** is recommended
- Maximum file size: **10 MB**
- Formats: **WAV**, **MP3**, **OGG**, and **OPUS**
- Only clone your own voice, or a voice you have permission to use

## How to clone your voice

1. Go to **Voice** from the sidebar, and find **Create a voice**
2. Keep **Voice clone** selected
3. Choose **Record Your Voice** or **Upload Audio**
4. If you are recording, choose your language and read the script on screen
5. Name your voice and create it
6. Assign the voice to a Double

## Recording tips

- Mic recommendations
  - Use a studio-quality mic or condenser mic if available
  - On mobile phones, use the Dolby app on Android or Voice Memos on iOS
- Record in a quiet room
- Speak at your normal pace and volume, and finish sentences naturally
- Make sure only one voice is heard
- Listen to the recording before submitting it

## Read the script

The recording screen shows scripts in the language you choose. Read naturally, like you are speaking to another person. Do not rush or perform.

In English:

> Hi, I'm recording this so my Double can learn my voice and sound like me. I speak clearly, naturally, and with a little warmth. Sometimes I'm calm, sometimes more energetic. Can you hear the difference? I explain ideas, answer questions, and keep things simple and easy to follow.

> Hi, this is my voice sample for The Double. I'm speaking in a calm and natural way, with clear words and small pauses. My Double should sound helpful, confident, and easy to talk to, just like I would in a real conversation.

## What to know

- The number of voice clones you can keep depends on your plan. [View pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
- Assign one voice to as many Doubles as you like

A clear recording produces the best result. If the clone sounds flat, noisy, or unnatural, try recording again in a quieter room.
