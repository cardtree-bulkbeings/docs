---
title: "Designing a Voice (Beta)"
---

Don't want to clone? Describe a voice, and your Double creates a sample for you to try.

## How to design a voice

1. Go to **Voice** from the sidebar, find **Create a voice**, and select **Voice design**
2. In **Describe the voice**, write 8 to 300 characters. Age, gender, accent, and tone work best
   - Example: "A mid-aged woman from the UK, warm and calm"
3. Click **Generate voice**
4. Click **Play** to listen to the result
5. Click **Create voice** to save it
6. Assign the voice to a Double

## What to know

- Use **View design history** to go back to a sample you generated earlier, and create a voice from it in one step
- You can generate up to 40 samples a day
- Designed voices count towards your plan's voice limit. [View pricing](https://thedouble.ai/pricing)
