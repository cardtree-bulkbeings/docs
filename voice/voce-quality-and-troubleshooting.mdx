---
title: "Voice Quality and Troubleshooting"
---

**Why cloning can fail**

- The recording has too much background noise
- The file is corrupted or recorded at very low quality
- An MP3 caused a processing error
- The sample is shorter than 6 seconds or longer than 30 seconds
- The file is larger than 10 MB

**How to fix a failed clone**

1. Re-record the sample in a quieter environment
2. Use a better microphone or a dedicated voice memo app
3. If you are using MP3, convert the file to WAV and re-upload
4. Read the script shown on screen
5. Delete the failed clone and create a new one

**If a language or model isn't available**

- Some languages work only on the **UHQ** model
- UHQ and HQ are for custom voices. Presets use their built-in model
- Hover over a greyed-out model to see why

**If a word sounds wrong**

- Add it under **Custom pronunciations**
- If your Double mishears it, add it under **Speech recognition**

**What to know**

- Clone quality follows recording quality. A poor recording can't be enhanced afterwards
- Cloning may occasionally fail on the first attempt. Try once more before changing format
