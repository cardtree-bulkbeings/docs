---
title: "Voice Overview"
---

Voice lets your AI Double speak in real-time conversations, using a cloned voice, a voice you design, or a preset voice from the library.

- **Voice** in the main sidebar is where you create voices and assign them to any Double
- **Your Double → Voice** is where you manage that Double's voice settings

### **What Voice does**

- Clones your voice from an uploaded or recorded audio sample
- Creates a brand-new voice from a text description
- Powers real-time voice conversations with visitors on any profile or embedded chatbot
- Speaks for your Double in Google Meet calls
- Works alongside chat. Both use the same knowledge, Persona, and behavior settings
- Supports 40\+ languages with automatic detection

### **Voice Models**

There are three voice models. Choose one under **Language & Models**.

| **Model** | **Best for** | **Available for** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UHQ** | Best quality and expression | Custom voices |
| **HQ** | High-quality voice, the default | Custom voices and HQ presets |
| **Lite** | Fast responses | Lite presets |

**Note:** Preset voices use their built-in model. UHQ and HQ are available for your custom voices. If a model is greyed out, hover over it to see why.

### **Choosing a Voice**

You have three options:

1. **Clone your own voice.** Record or upload a sample and create a voice that sounds like you
2. **Design a voice (Beta).** Describe the voice you want
3. **Pick a preset.** Choose an HQ or Lite voice from the Voice library

### **Testing your voice**

The **Current voice** panel on **Your Double → Voice** lets you type up to 20 words and hear them spoken. You can download the audio and find earlier samples under **Sample history**.

### **Default Voice Language**

Set the default language visitors start with when opening a voice chat. This is the language hint sent to speech recognition.

Go to **Your Double → Voice → Language & Models** and choose a language. English is set by default.

Visitors see the language flag preselected in voice chat. They can still switch languages manually during a conversation.

### **Expressive Mode**

Toggle **Expressive Mode** on to add subtle, human-like expression to your Double's voice, like natural pauses, hesitation, or laughter when it fits the conversation. Makes your Double sound less robotic and more like you.

Expressive Mode is off by default. Toggle it on from **Your Double → Voice**.

### **Multilingual Support**

Your Double speaks 40\+ languages automatically.

When a visitor starts a conversation, your Double detects their language and responds in the same language. No setup needed.

- **Automatically** (default): your Double identifies the language from the visitor's message and replies in kind
- **By choice:** visitors can click the language flag in the chat input to pick their preferred language

Supported languages: Arabic, Assamese, Bengali, Burmese, Chinese, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Gujarati, Hebrew, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Kannada, Khmer, Korean, Lao, Maithili, Malay, Malayalam, Marathi, Norwegian, Odia, Polish, Portuguese, Punjabi, Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tagalog, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Urdu, Vietnamese

**Note:** Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Maithili, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu work only on the **UHQ** model.

**What to know**

- Multilingual support works for both text chat and voice calls
- Visitors can switch languages mid-conversation
- Voice responses are delivered in the visitor's selected or detected language
- The default language set in Voice settings is the starting hint. Visitors can override it

### **Pronunciation**

Control how your Double says names, brands, acronyms, and other words without changing how they appear in the chat transcript.

1. Go to **Your Double → Voice**
2. Click **Custom pronunciations → Manage**
3. Click **Add pronunciation**
4. Enter the word or phrase
5. Enter how it should sound using simple phonetic spelling, for example `SAH-hith` or `hee-MAHN-shoo`
6. Click **Preview** to hear it, then click **Add**

**Note:** Pronunciation settings affect voice only. The original spelling is always shown in the chat transcript.

### **Speech Recognition**

Use **Speech recognition → Manage** to add words your Double should hear correctly, like names, brands, acronyms, and uncommon phrases.

### **Trigger Words**

Turn on **Trigger words** so your Double only responds when someone addresses it with one of your phrases. You can add up to 5.

**Note:** Google Meet has its own trigger words, under the channel's **Settings** tab.
